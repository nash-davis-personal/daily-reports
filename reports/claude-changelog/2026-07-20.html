Change 2.1.215 · Jul 19, 2026

Claude no longer runs /verify and /code-review on its own

Per the changelog: Claude no longer runs the /verify and /code-review skills on its own. Invoke them with /verify or /code-review when you want them.

This is the only bullet in the release. Nothing else shipped in this window.

So what for TSD: If you were relying on Claude to self-review before handing back a Next.js change on Bisque or Studio C, that safety net is gone unless you ask for it. Add an explicit /code-review step to your end-of-task habit on any client branch before it hits a Vercel preview. Upside: turns that used to silently burn tokens on unrequested review passes now finish faster and cheaper.
