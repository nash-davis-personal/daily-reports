◆ Patch this week (your stack)

Security next 15.5.28 / 16.4.1

Next.js October security release: the follow-up checklist

Yesterday's out-of-band release fixed two Critical RCEs and one High DoS. The first RCE hits 16.x apps with Cache Components where a use cache function returns typed arrays (GHSA-673q-mjm6-r5xr). The second is in Image Optimization when it processes SVGs through sharp/librsvg on glibc Linux (CVE-2026-96889), which also covers next/og ImageResponse. The DoS affects App Router Server Actions on 13.x–16.x (GHSA-p3m5-59mp-jv5r). The fixes ship in 15.5.28 and 16.4.1.

Two catches surfaced since. One security write-up says next@15.5.28 still accepts sharp ^0.34.3, so an existing lockfile can keep the vulnerable decoder; the fix needs sharp 0.35.5. Netlify also says old deploy previews and branch deploys stay vulnerable until they're deleted. Separately, endoflife.date lists security support for the Next.js 15 LTS line ending October 21.

So what for TSD: On every client repo: upgrade next, then run npm ls sharp (or check the lockfile) and confirm sharp 0.35.5 or later. Redeploy production, then delete or protect old preview deployments in Vercel. Put any site still on 15.x on a 16.4.1 upgrade list this week, because the security patches stop after Oct 21.

Security Oct 13–14

Microsoft's record Patch Tuesday and an exploited Ivanti zero-day

Microsoft's October update fixes 1,413 vulnerabilities, 1,102 of them in Windows and 134 in Office. The urgent one is CVE-2026-96951, a Mark-of-the-Web bypass that lets malicious downloads skip security warnings, and it is already being exploited. CISA gave federal agencies until October 16.

Ivanti disclosed CVE-2026-104935 in Connect Secure VPN on October 13. It's an authentication bypass rated 8.6, under active attack, and now patched. A separate privilege-escalation flaw was still unpatched at publication.

So what for TSD: For SMB clients whose Windows machines nobody manages, a one-line text saying "run Windows Update today and restart" is cheap goodwill, and it opens the door to a paid IT-hygiene check.

Platform Oct 13

Vercel and Supabase: small upgrades, plus a deadline coming up

Vercel's October 13 changelog adds Fish Audio's Drama 3 speech model and MAI-Image-2.6 (plus a Flash variant) to AI Gateway, and speeds up repository checkout and Git LFS downloads before builds automatically. Supabase announced Declarative Schemas 2.0 built on pg-delta for CLI 2.119+; we've only confirmed that through a third-party guide.

Still pending: from October 30, new public tables on existing Supabase projects stop being exposed to the Data API by default, so tables need explicit grants.

So what for TSD: No action needed on Vercel. Before Oct 30, list every Supabase project where code creates tables at runtime or through migrations and expects the Data API to see them, and add explicit grants to those migrations now.

◆ Chips and the AI buildout

Earnings Oct 15

TSMC posts a record Q3; ASML raises its outlook

TSMC reported Q3 revenue of US$46.72 billion, up 41.2% from a year earlier, with a 66.4% gross margin. High-performance computing made up 68% of revenue, 3nm accounted for 33% of wafer revenue and 2nm already 8%. The day before, ASML reported record Q3 sales of €11.7 billion and guided 2026 to €44–45 billion. It also says supply of its lithography machines will stay tight.

So what for TSD: Real chip demand is still rising even while AI stocks sell off. Model prices keep falling because capacity keeps growing, so keep building offers that get cheaper to deliver every quarter.
TSMC Q3 (Crypto Briefing) ASML IR
Markets Oct 14

CoreWeave's bonds touch 15% as AI-debt worries spread

CoreWeave's $1.25 billion of bonds due 2032 touched a 15% yield on October 13 before closing at 14.5%. On October 14 the stock fell about 15%, Nebius fell 11% and Oracle 9%. CoreWeave carried about $52 billion of debt at the end of June, and its Q2 interest expense was $640 million. The CEO told staff the market is acting "irrationally", and the company is looking at chip-backed loans from life insurers.

So what for TSD: No direct effect on your hosting. If AI-cloud financing gets tighter, the smaller GPU clouds take the hit first, so stick with Vercel, Supabase and the major model APIs.
Yahoo Finance

Apple closes above $5 trillion and launches a $299 home hub
