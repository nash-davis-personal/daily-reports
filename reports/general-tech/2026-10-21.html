Security Oct 14

Next.js and React: critical RCEs in use cache and SVG image optimization

Netlify's writeup lists a critical RCE in use cache functions returning typed arrays (GHSA-673q-mjm6-r5xr, Next.js 16.x with Cache Components), a critical RCE in Image Optimization with SVG files (GHSA-2464-fhr4-w9xw, CVE-2026-96889) and a high-severity Server Actions DoS (GHSA-p3m5-59mp-jv5r, App Router 13.x–16.x with at least one Server Action). Fixed versions are 15.5.28 and 16.4.1.

So what for TSD: Patch now. Run npm ls next on every client repo, bump to 16.4.1 or later (15.5.28 on the 15.x line), redeploy, and log it. See the Money Moves brief for how to turn this into a retainer.

Supabase Oct 12–20

Supabase: cipher retirement, adapter removal, and a production-check guide

Supabase will disable an older subset of TLS 1.2 (CBC-mode) ciphers on project HTTP APIs no earlier than Nov 10, and is contacting affected project owners directly. The Hono, H3, Elysia and NestJS adapters in @supabase/server are deprecated and will be removed on Dec 1. A blog post dated Oct 20 covers checking production systems with a coding agent.

So what for TSD: Grep client code for the deprecated adapters this week. Old HTTP clients or legacy devices on a client project could break on Nov 10.

◆ Chips and hardware

Memory Oct 15

Micron opens a second advanced packaging plant in Taichung

Digitimes reports Micron opened AATT2 to expand HBM packaging and testing. Micron expects memory and storage supply to tighten substantially in fiscal 2027 and 2028 and has agreements covering most of its 2027 HBM bit supply. A Singapore HBM packaging site should begin initial output in early 2027.

So what for TSD: Expect hardware, GPU-hosting and RAM-heavy dev machine prices to stay high. Favor hosted inference over buying hardware.

Foundry Oct 15–19

TSMC capacity is contested and Intel 18A is in mass production

CNBC reports Nvidia, Apple and Broadcom are in a "fierce" battle for TSMC capacity. Japan approved revised Kumamoto plans with subsidies up to JPY645.15 billion, switching the second fab to 3nm with first shipments in Q4 2028. Digitimes says Intel's 18A process has entered mass production. A report of roughly 10% Intel and AMD price hikes comes from a single source and is unconfirmed.

So what for TSD: Background for client conversations about AI cost: compute supply is the constraint, so per-token discounts like Gemini Argon's promo may not last.
