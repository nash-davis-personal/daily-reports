◆ Patch and upgrade

Deadline Oct 21

Next.js 15 support ends Wednesday; nothing new to patch since Oct 14

No new Next.js, React, Vercel or Supabase advisory surfaced since the October 14 security release, which fixed two critical bugs (a use cache remote-code-execution issue with Cache Components and an SVG image-optimization RCE through sharp) and a high-severity Server Actions denial of service. Fixed versions are 16.4.1 and 15.5.28. The 15.x line drops out of Maintenance LTS on October 21, so the next monthly security release will only patch 16.

So what for TSD: Patch instruction: confirm every client repo is on 16.4.1 or later (16.4.2 is fine). Any repo still on 15.x needs its upgrade branch merged and deployed by Tuesday night. Leave the proxy.ts pattern alone during the move.

Security Oct 12–14

GitLab patches a CVSS 10 code-execution bug in its CI/CD regex engine

CVE-2026-97229 is an integer overflow in GitLab's CI/CD regular-expression compiler. A crafted regex in a pipeline config lets an unauthenticated attacker run code on the GitLab server. Fixed in 19.4.2, 19.3.5 and 19.2.8, alongside 23 other fixes. GitLab.com and Dedicated are already patched; Singapore's CSA told self-managed users to patch immediately on Oct 14.

So what for TSD: TSD runs on GitHub, so no action for you. If a client or a client's previous agency hosts its own GitLab, tell them to patch this weekend. It's a quick credibility win in a discovery call too.

◆ Your stack

Vercel Oct 16–17

AI Gateway adds Reflection AI's Beam, free during launch

Vercel added Beam, Reflection AI's US-made open-weight reasoning model, to AI Gateway. It has 501B parameters with 23B active, a 1M-token context, tool calling, structured outputs and adjustable reasoning effort, under Apache 2.0. It's free "for a limited time"; no end date or limits were stated. Use reflection/beam to roll onto paid pricing automatically when the window closes, or reflection/beam-free to have it stop serving instead. Gateway also added DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (1017 build), Perplexity's Decider V1.2 and Elorian's Glimpse.

So what for TSD: Run your internal evals and batch content jobs on reflection/beam-free while it's free. Keep it off client production: the free slug just stops, and Apache 2.0 makes it a clean license if it earns a paid slot later.

◆ Agents meet small business

Platforms Oct 15–16

Wix agents can pay suppliers, Webflow plugs into Gemini, PayPal checkout goes into Glance

Symphony by Wix, its multi-agent platform for small businesses, now connects to Stripe's Link wallet so agents can reorder stock or pay invoices. The owner approves each purchase, and Stripe pays with a token or one-time virtual card so the agent never holds card details. Webflow became a connected app inside Google Gemini through its MCP server, free to install, with existing roles and activity logs applied. PayPal checkout now works inside Glance without a redirect, and Lightspeed opened an approval-gated agent beta for retailers.

So what for TSD: Wix and Webflow are TSD's direct competition for small-business sites, and they now sell "an agent that runs errands for you." Your answer is the same pattern built custom: approval-by-text before any money moves, logged actions, scoped credentials. Stripe's Link-for-agents is the piece to look at for a client who wants reorders automated.

Traffic Oct 15–16

Agent traffic jumps 41.5% in a quarter; Meta's Muse is a third of it

DataDome's Q3 report counts nearly 25 billion AI-agent requests across its network from July through September, up 41.5% on Q2. Meta's Muse agent was 33% of agentic browser requests in September after only nine days in market, almost all from cloud servers. DataDome says 8.9% of sampled Muse requests touched form, login, payment, sign-up or cart endpoints, and stresses that measures exposure, not confirmed abuse. These are DataDome's own numbers. Separately, Proof is demoing x401, an open protocol for a site to ask an agent who it represents, at Money20/20 Oct 18–21.

So what for TSD: Expect booking forms and contact forms to get agent submissions that look human. Check Vercel's bot protection and firewall settings on client sites with forms or carts, rate-limit form endpoints, and decide whether a legit agent booking (a customer's assistant) should get through. That decision is a client conversation.
