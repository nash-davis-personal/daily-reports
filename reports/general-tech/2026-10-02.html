◆ Your stack

Security status checked Oct 2

No new advisories for Next.js, React, Tailwind or Supabase; 16.3.8 and 15.5.27 remain the patched versions

The GitHub release and advisory feeds show nothing newer than the Sept. 30 Next.js security release. The latest stable versions are Next.js 16.3.8 and 15.5.27, React 19.3.0 (Sept. 9), Tailwind 4.3.3 (July 16) and supabase-js 2.117.2 (Sept. 25). Vite shipped 8.3.2 on Oct. 1, a routine patch release. The Sept. 30 Next.js advisories included a high-severity server-side request forgery in Image Optimization, and the critical next/og remote-code-execution advisory from Sept. 22 still applies to anything older.

So what for TSD: Today's job is finishing Wednesday's. List every client repo's Next.js version and confirm each one reads 16.3.8 or 15.5.27. A site still on 16.3.7 or lower is exposed to a published SSRF.

Supabase Oct 1

Supabase moved OrioleDB to public beta and opened it to paid plans

OrioleDB is Supabase's alternative storage engine for Postgres. Projects using it can now be created on Pro, Team and Enterprise plans and are billed the same as standard Postgres projects. The beta adds compute and disk resizing, pause and restore, version upgrades, read replicas and scheduled backups. New projects run OrioleDB beta18 on Postgres 17.11.

Supabase says the beta is for testing, recommends against production use and offers no SLA. Point-in-time recovery and restoring a backup into a new project are unavailable.

So what for TSD: Keep every client on standard Postgres. No point-in-time recovery rules it out for anything that holds bookings or customer records. It is worth one throwaway project to see whether Concierge's vector queries run faster.

Vercel Sept 30 – Oct 1

Vercel's Sandbox can join a Secure Compute network, and the AI Gateway added web tools and audio models

On Sept. 30 Vercel let Sandboxes connect to a dedicated Secure Compute network, gave Vercel Agent the ability to install private npm packages using team environment variables, and added Browserbase Search and Fetch as tools any gateway model can call. On Oct. 1 the gateway added Microsoft's new speech and transcription models and two more models, one of them free through Oct. 31. The two changes covered here yesterday still stand: the CDN no longer stores responses whose Vary header includes Cookie, and Edge Requests are now called CDN Requests with no price change.

So what for TSD: Search and fetch through the gateway means Concierge could look up a live page without you running a scraper. The Microsoft transcription model is the one to test for Front Desk, covered in today's AI brief.

◆ Platforms and security

Platform Oct 1 – 2

Reddit will end RSS on Nov. 13 and close its public API by March 2027

Reddit says RSS feeds are being abused for large-scale scraping and automation, and it will stop serving them on Nov. 13. It stops accepting new public API requests on Oct. 31. Developers of approved third-party apps and bots must register them by Jan. 12, 2027, and public API access closes in March 2027, with Reddit steering developers to its Developer Platform. Commercial access will require a deal with Reddit. Old Reddit will be limited to logged-in moderators and people who used the classic interface in the past 90 days.

The change hits social-listening tools, research projects and AI assistants that read Reddit to answer questions.

So what for TSD: Check whether any scout routine, lead-monitoring script or client tool reads a Reddit .rss URL or the public API. Anything that does stops working Nov. 13. It is also a reminder to build on data the client owns, such as their reviews, their inbox and their call logs.

Exploited Sept 30

CISA added an exploited Cisco SD-WAN Manager login bypass to its must-patch list, due Oct. 3

CVE-2026-76504, rated 9.8, lets an unauthenticated attacker reach the admin API of Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager through a URL-encoding flaw in an HTTP request. Cisco confirms attacks are under way. CISA listed it on Sept. 30 and gave federal agencies until Oct. 3. Rapid7 advises upgrading to a fixed release and searching logs for POST requests to encoded variants of "/j_security_check".

So what for TSD: This is not on your stack and needs no action from you. If a client with multiple locations mentions a Cisco SD-WAN, forward their IT contact the Rapid7 link.

◆ Chips, networks and space

Telecom this week

AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon formed a joint venture to cover dead zones by satellite

The three carriers signed a joint-venture agreement to offer direct-to-device satellite connectivity where cell towers don't reach, and to set common technical specifications. It follows their May plan to pool spectrum for satellite service. Paul Roth, a former AT&T and Cingular executive, is interim CEO. A board with members from each carrier will run it, and each company keeps its existing satellite agreements.

So what for TSD: Rural coverage is a real gap for trades and mobile businesses, including detailing routes outside the metro. Nothing to build yet. Once phones hold a signal everywhere, missed-call and text-back tools reach more of a field crew's day.

Space Oct 1

Google's first Project Suncatcher satellite launched with four AI chips aboard

The refrigerator-size prototype rode a SpaceX Falcon 9 from Vandenberg on the Transporter-18 rideshare and reached low Earth orbit, according to the Tech Startups roundup. It carries four Trillium-generation TPUs on about 1 kilowatt of solar power and will process short Gemini queries in roughly 15-minute windows before shutting down to cool. The flight tests whether the chips survive. Google plans a pair of TPU satellites with Planet Labs in 2027 to test the laser links a real cluster would need. The full design calls for 81 satellites flying within a kilometer of each other.

So what for TSD: No effect on anything you ship. It shows how far the search for AI power has gone, and that is why cloud inference pricing is worth watching before you lock in a multi-year client quote.

Export controls Oct 1

The Justice Department arrested a California reseller accused of smuggling over $300 million in GPU servers to China

Greg Lui, 38, of San Gabriel, ran Earthmade Computer Inc. Prosecutors say that in 2023 and 2024 he bought export-restricted servers with Nvidia GPUs from US manufacturers using paperwork that named other destinations, shipped them to Malaysia and Singapore through freight forwarders, and had them rerouted to China. They say Malaysia-based companies paid him $176 million between January and October 2024. He is charged with conspiracy to violate the Export Control Reform Act, smuggling and conspiracy to commit money laundering. The charges are allegations.

So what for TSD: No direct effect. It is a measure of how scarce high-end GPUs remain, and scarcity is what keeps model prices from falling faster.
