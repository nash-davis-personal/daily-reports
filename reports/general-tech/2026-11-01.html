◆ Your stack

Status as of Nov 1

No new security advisories since the Oct 14 batch; Next.js 16.5 is still in canary

Next.js's GitHub advisory list still tops out with the Oct 14 pair of critical unauthenticated RCEs (the use cache typed-array bug and SVG image optimization). React's last releases were the Oct 14 security patches (19.0.9, 19.1.10, 19.2.9). Next.js pushed 16.5.0-canary.35 and canary.36 on Oct 31 with no stable release. Tailwind is unchanged at 4.3.3, and Supabase's CLI is working through 3.0 betas (beta.17 on Oct 30).

So what for TSD: Nothing new to install. Run npm ls next react-server-dom-webpack sharp on each client repo and confirm Next is at or above the Oct 14 fixed line (16.4.1+ or 15.5.28+) and sharp is 0.35.5+. Keep the Supabase CLI 3.0 beta out of client deploy scripts until it ships stable.

Check Nov 1, 2 a.m.

Daylight saving time ended overnight, and the 1 a.m. hour happened twice

US clocks went back an hour at 2:00 a.m. on Sunday, Nov 1. Code that stores local times without a time zone, or adds 24 hours to get "tomorrow," can now send a reminder an hour off, run a job twice, or show the wrong opening hours.

So what for TSD: Spot-check Booking Bridge and any reminder or cron jobs this morning. Make sure times are stored in UTC with an IANA zone like America/New_York, and compare one Monday appointment's confirmation against its calendar entry. Vercel cron runs in UTC, so a "9 a.m." job is now an hour off local time unless you adjusted it.

◆ Security and chips

Fixed Oct 31

The ChatGPT share-link bug chained a trusted script host from its CSP

Parameter's exploit used three parts: a third-party library's message listener that accepted messages from any sender, a YouTube-hosted script the content security policy allowed, and a preview feature. OpenAI fixed it within eight days. Full details are in today's General AI brief.

So what for TSD: Allowing a big host in your CSP isn't automatically safe. On client sites with video or map embeds, give those hosts frame-src only and keep them out of script-src unless the site needs them there.
