◆ Patch this first

Exploited CVE-2026-60004 ◆ Aug 26

A Gitea RCE went from disclosure to active exploitation in under a month

CVE-2026-60004 carries a CVSS of 9.8. An attacker holding ordinary write access to any repository can plant an executable Git hook and run arbitrary shell commands with the privileges of the Gitea service account. Observed attacks execute inside the Gitea container as the git user, first writing a proof of code execution back into a Git branch, then pulling a universal shell loader followed by a crypto-miner-style dropper.

CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on August 25 and told federal civilian agencies to remediate by August 28. Separately, CVE-2026-59774 is an unauthenticated arbitrary file read in the repository markup-rendering feature, reachable by sending a crafted request to POST /{owner}/{repo}/markup on any suitable public repo. It affects Gitea 1.22.1 through 1.27.0, is fixed in 1.27.1, and the useful targets are exactly the ones you would guess: app.ini, deployment metadata, OAuth credentials, JWT secrets and database settings.

So what for TSD: You are on GitHub, so your own repos are fine. The exposure is client-side: any SMB you inherited that self-hosts Gitea on a VPS, and any legacy box a previous developer left running. Spend fifteen minutes today — grep your client notes for "gitea", and for anyone running it, upgrade to 1.27.1 or later, then rotate every credential in app.ini on the assumption the file already leaked. Write-access-equals-shell means a contractor account from three years ago is a live foothold. This is also a free, non-salesy reason to email dormant clients, and those emails start more retainer conversations than a newsletter does.

Vercel Aug 26, 2026

Security Dashboard is generally available, and Python projects got routing rules

Vercel's August 26 changelog carries six entries. The Security Dashboard reached general availability, giving one view of security posture across every project on the account. Python projects — FastAPI, Django, Flask — gained routing rules, so response headers and request rewrites can be changed without redeploying.

The other four entries are model additions to AI Gateway: Meta's Muse Image, Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, Qwen 3.8 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash, all behind a single API key with automatic fallbacks and spend tracking. That follows August 25, when Speed Insights got a free tier on every plan and Vercel confirmed hosted apps were protected from the August Next.js vulnerabilities.

So what for TSD: Open the Security Dashboard once for every project you host and screenshot the result. That screenshot is the cheapest possible artifact for a Managed AI retainer check-in, and it costs you one click per client per month. The Python routing rules matter if you have been keeping transcription or media jobs on a separate box — a small FastAPI service on Vercel is now closer to a first-class citizen, which removes one reason to pay for a second host.

◆ Silicon and the money behind it

Chips Aug 25–26, 2026

OpenAI's Jalapeño beat Blackwell on performance per watt, with an asterisk

OpenAI presented the first public results for its in-house inference accelerator at Hot Chips on August 25, measured on SemiAnalysis's InferenceX benchmark, which scores throughput, power draw and latency across the full request pipeline rather than raw FLOPs. The headline claims are 1.5 to 1.9 times more compute per watt and 1.7 to 3.6 times lower latency than Nvidia Blackwell systems. On GPT-OSS 120B the chip delivered 85,448 mixed TPS per kilowatt against 44,960 for a GB200.

The asterisk is memory. Jalapeño uses HBM4 and Blackwell does not, so analysts noted the fair comparison is Nvidia's Rubin platform, which also uses HBM4. OpenAI says it will start deploying the chip in its own infrastructure by the end of 2026, and has said it keeps buying from Nvidia regardless.

So what for TSD: Watts per token is becoming the unit that sets your API bill, and every major buyer is now building silicon to escape one vendor's margin. For you that means inference prices keep drifting down and you should not sign a client to a multi-year fixed price with today's token cost baked in. Price retainers on outcomes — calls answered, bookings captured — and let falling inference cost widen your margin instead of triggering a renegotiation.

Legal Aug 26, 2026

Meta agreed to pay up to $16.68 billion and to cap teen usage by default

The settlement resolves claims that Facebook and Instagram were designed to keep teenagers engaged. $12.7 billion goes to states over ten years for youth safety programs, and a further $5.3 billion is contingent on YouTube and TikTok adopting comparable limits — an unusual structure that makes part of the payment depend on competitors' behavior.

The product commitments are the operative part: a two-hour daily cap for under-18 accounts, blocked usage between midnight and 6am, muted notifications during school hours, and stronger age verification.

So what for TSD: Stronger age verification on the two biggest ad platforms means reach among under-18 audiences gets noisier and more expensive to target. That barely touches your book, which is trades, salons and specialty retail selling to adults. What does touch you is precedent: default-on protective settings and verifiable age signals are heading toward any site with accounts. If you build a client portal, put a date-of-birth field and a sane default in the schema now rather than migrating it later.

Funding Aug 26, 2026

Gatik raised $200 million for driverless middle-mile freight

The Series D was led by the Qatar Investment Authority, joined by Koch Disruptive Technologies, Millennium Management, ARK Invest and Intact Private Capital. Gatik runs driverless box trucks on fixed short-haul routes between distribution centers and stores, and reports 85,000 fully driverless orders completed against $600 million in contracted revenue.

Alongside it, XPeng's robotics unit closed more than $900 million at a post-money valuation above $6.3 billion, led by IDG Capital with Tencent and Alibaba as strategic investors — the largest single private round in China's embodied-AI sector, with IRON humanoid mass production targeted for the end of 2026. Nvidia also introduced the Jetson Orin Nano 2 for on-device AI in drones and small robots.

So what for TSD: Contracted revenue on fixed repeatable routes is why Gatik is fundable while general autonomy is not — narrow scope, same route every day, measurable. That is the exact argument for how you should scope TSD Front Desk: one repeated job done reliably beats a general assistant that occasionally impresses. Sell the narrow version, name the routes it runs.
