◆ Security

Fixed Disclosed September 24

A Cloudflare Containers bug let one customer read another's deleted data

When a container was deleted, its 64KB thin-provisioned disk blocks went back into a shared pool without being wiped. Researchers who spun up new containers recovered other customers' .env files, SQLite databases and credential files on 18 of 24 attempts. The bug was reported September 4 and fully fixed September 19.

Cloudflare says customers don't need to do anything and it found no exploitation beyond the researchers' own tests.

So what for TSD: Nothing to patch, but if any TSD or client project ran in Cloudflare Containers before September 19 with secrets baked into the image or written to disk, rotate those keys. The broader rule for client work: keep secrets in the platform's secret store (Vercel env vars, Wrangler secrets), never in files on a container's disk.
The Hacker News — Cloudflare fixes flaw that let one customer read another's data
Breach September 25

Bitget loses $351.6M in a suspected North Korean hack

Attackers got into a backend wallet system, fabricated transaction data and triggered Bitget's own authorization process to move ETH, XRP, BNB, AVAX, USDT and USDC out of hot and warm wallets. The CEO says customer balances are intact and cold storage is safe; withdrawals are paused. Mandiant and SlowMist are investigating, and several chain foundations froze the attacker's addresses.

So what for TSD: The attack didn't break the approval system; it fed the system forged inputs. Any client automation where an AI agent or webhook can trigger refunds, payouts or bookings needs the same check: verify the source data independently before the action fires, and cap what one automated action can move.
The Hacker News — Bitget suspected North Korean hack
Exploited September 24–25

CISA flags exploited WSO2 and Adobe Commerce flaws; WordPress and Roundcube bugs are live too

CISA added CVE-2026-5430 (CVSS 9.8), a path-traversal-to-RCE bug in WSO2 API Manager and Gateway exploited since September 13, and CVE-2026-71362 (CVSS 9.1), an authorization flaw in Adobe Commerce and Magento, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list. Federal agencies must patch by September 27.

Separately, WordPress CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2 RCE) was reportedly exploited within hours of disclosure, and a Roundcube pre-auth SQL injection (CVE-2026-48842, fixed in 1.6.16 and 1.7.1 back in May) is now under active attack, with about 523,000 exposed instances.

So what for TSD: This is a prospecting list. Local SMBs on WordPress or Magento are exposed this week. Grant and Bishop can open with "your site platform had a critical bug exploited within hours; here's a free check," and the migration pitch sells itself: a Next.js site with owned code and no plugin surface.

◆ Your stack

Feature September 23–24

Vercel lifted the Blob store limit and put Sandbox Drives in public beta

Vercel's changelog: unlimited Blob stores on every plan, Sandbox Drives in public beta with up to 16 TiB of storage, and TanStack AI support in Vercel Connect. Next.js shipped only canaries (16.4.0-canary.40 through .45) with no security fixes. Supabase's last changelog entry is September 21, when Replication was renamed Pipelines.

So what for TSD: Unlimited Blob stores means one store per client project, which keeps each client's uploads separable when you hand over the code. Make that the default in the project starter. No upgrades required today.

Deal September 24

Databricks bought Row Zero, a spreadsheet that handles a million-plus rows

Row Zero had raised $10M at about a $40M valuation; the price wasn't disclosed. It will plug into Databricks' Genie agent. It's Databricks' fourth acquisition this year, after a $5B raise in August and at a $7B revenue run rate.

So what for TSD: Big platforms are buying "the spreadsheet your ops person already lives in, plus an agent." The SMB version is a small Supabase-backed dashboard with a Claude chat over the client's own bookings and sales. That's a sellable add-on to any existing client.

◆ Space & robotics

New September 24–25

Google is putting four TPUs in orbit for Project Suncatcher

The test satellite, built with Planet, carries four TPUs and about 1,000W of solar power. It will run AI workloads in roughly 15-minute bursts, pausing to shed heat, for about a year. It rides SpaceX's Transporter-18 from Vandenberg, reported for about October 1. A two-satellite laser-link test is planned for 2027.

So what for TSD: No near-term effect. It's a marker for where AI compute costs may head as power becomes the constraint on the ground.
Technology.org — Project Suncatcher TPU orbit test
Scale September 24

Waymo runs about 4,000 robotaxis and 500,000 paid rides a week

Waymo now operates in 15 US cities, up from 3 in September 2024. Its Texas fleet is 1,102 vehicles, up nearly half in three weeks, and about a third are the Zeekr-built "Ojai" minivans. MoffettNathanson projects 5,100 Ojais imported by year-end.

So what for TSD: Charlotte isn't on the list yet. When it lands, local businesses near pickup zones will want "robotaxi drop-off" directions and booking links on their sites. Small, but an easy upsell to have ready.
