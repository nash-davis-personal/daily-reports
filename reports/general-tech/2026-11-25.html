◆ Security first

No action Nov 25

No new advisory for Next.js, React, Tailwind or Supabase; the Nov 3 fixes are still the line

The Next.js security release of Nov 3 fixed seven issues in 16.4.5 and 15.5.29, the worst a high-severity cross-tenant cache leak when unstable_cache and use cache are combined. Next.js 14 gets no fix. The React Server Components RCE (CVE-2026-126187) was patched Oct 14 and its record was last updated Nov 21. No new item for Tailwind or Supabase turned up in this window.

So what for TSD: Run a quick check that every client repo reports next@16.4.5 or 15.5.29 or later. Anything still on 14.x is unpatched and is an upgrade to quote.
Next.js advisories Netlify summary RSC RCE notes
Exploited Nov 24

A Drupal installer RCE found by Anthropic's scanner is under attack and has a Nov 27 federal deadline

CVE-2026-132165 lets an unauthenticated attacker, under certain conditions, rerun Drupal's install.php over a live site and take it over. Drupal shipped fixes on Nov 18 in 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22 and says exploitation began on Nov 19. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list on Nov 24 with a Nov 27 deadline for federal agencies. Drupal's advice: patch, block access to the installer, and check logs for install.php requests since Nov 18. Victim counts are not public.

So what for TSD: TSD does not run Drupal, but SMB prospects do. A prospect site on unpatched Drupal is an urgent, easy opener: offer a same-day patch, or a rebuild onto Next.js that removes the class of problem.
SecurityWeek Rescana
Research Nov 24

Researchers take over five Treasury subdomains for about $3 each through dangling DNS records

Intruder researchers cycled roughly 450,000 AWS Elastic IPs in 48 hours to catch addresses that old DNS records still pointed at. They claimed 28 such IPs across their targets, including five treasury.gov subdomains.

So what for TSD: When a client site moves to Vercel, delete the old A records that pointed at the previous host. Add a DNS cleanup line to TSD's launch checklist.
Intruder (via AI Weekly)

◆ Your platforms

Vercel Nov 24

Vercel adds an Audited ZDR tier on AI Gateway; Baseten, Modal and Wafer are first in line

A provider will carry the Audited ZDR label only after an independent firm tests its zero-retention claim against code, infrastructure and live traffic and publishes the report. Audits are under way; Vercel expects the first reports around Dec 8 or 9, and no provider qualifies until then. The move follows reports of prompts with planted credentials leaking from services that claimed no retention.

So what for TSD: For clients who worry about where their customer data goes (medical, legal, finance), this gives TSD a checkable answer. Once reports publish, route those clients' AI calls through audited providers and say so in the proposal.
Vercel blog The New Stack
Supabase Nov 24

Supabase opens a knowledge base written for developers and their coding agents

The public beta holds 237 troubleshooting articles, 12 migration guides and 11 best-of-2026 rundowns, structured so agents can read and apply them. It lands a week before the Dec 1 removal of the Hono, H3, Elysia and NestJS adapters from @supabase/server.

So what for TSD: Point Claude Code at it when doing the Dec 1 adapter migrations. Check every client repo for those four adapters before Monday.
Sulat Supabase changelog

◆ Big tech and hardware

Reliability Nov 24

Microsoft declares an internal Code Red after the Nov 12-13 Microsoft 365 outage

Memos seen by Reuters say the outage traced to an undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July and hit Exchange, Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide. The five-part plan covers load shedding, splitting traffic by region, faster recovery and AI agents to help diagnose failures, with a goal of one-hour global resolution.

So what for TSD: SMB clients felt that outage in their inboxes. It is a fair talking point for keeping a client's booking and lead capture on their own site, where one vendor's outage cannot take it down.
Claims Journal (Reuters)
