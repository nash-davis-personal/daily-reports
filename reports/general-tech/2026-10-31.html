◆ Security: patch and check this weekend

Breach Oct 29–30

Binance says a compromised engineer's laptop led to a $6.3 billion theft, the largest crypto heist on record

Binance's early investigation points to a DevOps engineer's laptop that gave the attacker access to a service container, which then submitted unauthorized withdrawals. Roughly $3B has been frozen on-chain, with Tether and Circle freezing about $1.57B and more than $800M respectively, per Reuters via Fox Business. About $3.3B sits in wallets that can't be frozen.

Deposits and withdrawals were paused. Binance says it will absorb the loss, and Binance.US says it was not affected. TRM Labs links the attack to a North Korean state group.

So what for TSD: Same lesson as the Claude Code extension: the developer's laptop holds the keys. Put production secrets for client projects behind short-lived tokens, keep Vercel and Supabase service keys out of shell profiles, and turn on hardware-key 2FA for GitHub and Vercel this weekend.

Patch now Oct 29–30

Trojanized Claude Code extension on Cursor's marketplace: confirm you are on 2.1.314

Version 2.1.313, published under Anthropic's real extension ID on Cursor's marketplace for about 11 hours on Oct 29, added an attacker SSH key, a reverse tunnel and a macOS LaunchAgent, and sent back your git email and the names of secret-looking environment variables. Cursor puts the maximum exposure at 5,000 users. Details are in today's General AI brief.

So what for TSD: Patch instruction: open Cursor, confirm the Claude Code extension is 2.1.314 or newer, run grep svc-telemetry ~/.ssh/authorized_keys, and check ~/Library/LaunchAgents. Any hit means rotating every token on that machine.
The Hacker News General AI brief
Advisory Oct 30

CISA adds two actively exploited Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its catalog

CVE-2026-111357 is missing authentication for a critical function and CVE-2026-118527 is predictable identifier generation. Both affect Ivanti Connect Secure, the VPN gateway, and CISA says both are being exploited.

So what for TSD: Not your stack, but some clients' offices run Ivanti VPNs. If a client mentions one, tell their IT contact to patch it this week. It's a goodwill touch that costs a text.

◆ Chips, platforms and hardware

Markets Oct 30

Intel falls 28.6% and chip stocks sink into a bear market

Intel guided fourth-quarter revenue to $15.0–16.0B on sluggish PC sales and fell 28.6%, its worst session in at least 40 years. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost 7.2% and the iShares Semiconductor ETF closed 29% below its recent high. Amazon's negative free cash flow and Alphabet's ad miss fed the wider worry about AI spending.

So what for TSD: Good for buyers. Weak PC demand and nervous chip makers usually mean better hardware deals heading into the holidays, so price any client hardware (POS tablets, office machines) after Black Friday. No effect on your hosting costs yet.

Autonomy Oct 30

Waymo's next robotaxi will be a Toyota bZ4X built in Japan

Toyota will build 2027 bZ4X EVs with Waymo's driving system integrated at its Takaoka plant. Testing starts in Japan next year, with UK deployment planned later.

So what for TSD: No direct work here. File it for the Charlotte pitch deck: robotaxis keep spreading, and local service businesses near airports and hotels will want booking flows that handle riders who arrive without a car.

Stack watch Oct 23–31

Your stack: no new stable releases, Next.js 16.5 in canary, and Supabase's grants rule is now live everywhere

Next.js 16.4.3 (Oct 23) is still the newest stable release; v16.5.0 canaries shipped Oct 30. React's last patches were 19.0.9, 19.1.10 and 19.2.9 on Oct 14, and Tailwind is still on 4.3.3. Supabase's change that stops auto-exposing new public-schema tables to the Data API reached every project on Oct 30.

So what for TSD: Nothing to patch on the framework side. The Supabase rule is the one to watch: any table you create from today needs explicit grants, or the client app gets empty results with no error. Add the grant to your migration template.
