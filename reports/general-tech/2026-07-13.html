Patch check CVE-2026-23869 · disclosed Apr 8

No new Next.js/Vercel advisory — but the April DoS is still the one to verify

Nothing new hit Nash's stack this window. The load-bearing item remains CVE-2026-23869, the high-severity (CVSS 7.5) React Server Components denial-of-service where a crafted HTTP request to any App Router Server Function endpoint can trigger runaway CPU on deserialization. It affects Next.js 13.x through 16.x on the App Router and is fixed in 15.5.15 (for 15.0.0+) and 16.2.3 (for 16.0.0+). Vercel's WAF auto-mitigates hosted projects, but Vercel's own guidance is not to rely on the WAF — the upgrade is the fix.

So what for TSD: Two-minute job. Run npx next --version across every client repo; anything on the App Router below 15.5.15 or 16.2.3 gets bumped and redeployed today. Self-hosted client apps (not on Vercel's WAF) are the priority — they have no automatic mitigation.

Markets Mon, Jul 13

Chip stocks give back Friday's record-listing pop

The semiconductor trade cooled hard to start the week. US-listed shares of SK Hynix fell about 8% Monday after Friday's Nasdaq debut — the largest-ever US listing by a foreign company at $26.5B raised — sent them soaring. Micron dropped roughly 5% and SanDisk about 6% alongside it. Index futures were mixed to modestly higher early, but memory and storage names led the pullback.

So what for TSD: No direct action. It is a reminder that the AI-infrastructure trade is now volatile enough to swing 8% in a session, which is the backdrop for why clients are cost-conscious right now. Nash's edge is selling the outcome, so the underlying chip price swings do not touch his margins.

Funding Filed Jul 2

Kling AI banks ~$2.8B to chase Sora

Kuaishou disclosed in a Hong Kong exchange filing that investors committed over 19B yuan (~$2.8B) to its Kling AI video unit at a ~$15B pre-money valuation, with the round capped near $3B and a post-money around $18B. Backers include Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu, and Abu Dhabi's BlueFive Capital. Reporting pegged Kling's annualized revenue climbing from roughly $240M to $500M in three months, and the raise dilutes the parent's stake to about 68%.

So what for TSD: AI video generation is now a real, revenue-backed market, not a demo. Cheap, capable video and image tools (Kling, plus Google's Video Remix and Meta's Muse) put a "short promo clip / social reel" add-on within reach for TSD's existing web clients — Nash already has the media pipeline to package and deliver it.
