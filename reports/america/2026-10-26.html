◆ Economy and markets

Markets Oct 26, pre-market

Futures climb after two losing weeks as Brent slips back to about $114

As of 5:31 a.m. ET, S&P 500 futures were up 0.31%, Nasdaq 100 futures 0.25% and Dow futures 0.51%. Last week the S&P 500 fell 0.47% and the Nasdaq 0.71%, its second straight weekly loss, while the Dow gained 0.41%. Friday's decline came with rising oil and worries about debt-funded AI spending.

Brent was at $114.34 (down 2.04%) and WTI at $100.75 (down 2.44%) after Friday's jump to a $116.72 settle, the highest close since April. Reports tie Friday's rise to Middle East tensions, including attacks on Saudi Arabia, and note that traders now worry prices this high will cut demand.

Why it matters: Oil above $100 feeds straight into fuel, shipping and airfare costs. For anyone managing their own money, the week's moves will come from Wednesday and Thursday's events more than from Monday's open.
TipRanks, Oct 26 ◆ US News / Reuters, Oct 26
Fed Decision Wed, Oct 28

The Fed decides Wednesday: markets expect a hold, one big bank expects a hike

Fed funds futures imply about a 90% chance the Fed holds its target range at 3.75%–4%, according to CNBC's reading of the market. Bank of America expects a quarter-point increase, which it calls a "heavy lift." The 10-year Treasury yield finished last week near 5%.

Why it matters: Most of the market has priced a hold, so a surprise hike would move mortgage, auto-loan and business-credit rates quickly. Borrowers shopping for a fixed rate have a reason to lock before Wednesday afternoon.
TipRanks citing CNBC, Oct 26
Calendar Oct 27 – Oct 29

Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon report this week, alongside PCE inflation and jobless claims

Bloomberg frames the Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon reports as a test of whether roughly $800B in AI capital spending is paying off. Visa, Mastercard, Intel, Boeing, PayPal, Starbucks, Exxon Mobil and Chevron also report. The data calendar: ADP employment and September durable-goods orders Tuesday, then the PCE price index and weekly jobless claims Thursday, alongside the advance read on third-quarter GDP.

Why it matters: Big Tech is a large share of most index funds and 401(k)s, and PCE is the inflation gauge the Fed watches most. Together they'll set the tone for rates and retirement balances going into November.

◆ Weather and public safety

Weather Oct 25

More flooding across the Four Corners after days of storms and at least one tornado

Days of heavy rain brought more flooding to the Four Corners region, where the National Weather Service has a flood watch in effect. At least one tornado touched down in New Mexico. Many residents remain evacuated and without power. Officials report no known deaths.

Why it matters: October flooding in the desert Southwest is unusual, and burn scars there flood fast. Anyone with property, travel or shipments in the region should watch NWS Albuquerque, Grand Junction and Flagstaff for updates.
AP News Summary, Oct 25
Safety Oct 25

Fire at an Oregon seafood plant forces evacuations over ammonia fears

A fire at a Pacific Seafood plant in Newport, Oregon, prompted evacuations because officials feared the smoke contained ammonia. The evacuation zone was reduced after officials found the ammonia tanks stable. No civilians were injured.

Why it matters: Many food plants use ammonia refrigeration, which is why a routine-looking fire can trigger a wide evacuation. A local event with a contained outcome.
