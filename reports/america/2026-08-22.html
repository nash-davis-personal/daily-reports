◆ The economy

Data Aug 21, 2026

Flash PMI showed the fastest business expansion in four years, carried by services

S&P Global's flash US composite PMI rose to 56.0 in August from 54.5 in July, against a consensus of 53.2. It is the second consecutive month of accelerating growth and the fastest pace since April 2022. Services did the lifting, at 56.8 from 54.6, the sharpest services expansion since December 2024.

Manufacturing went the other way. The headline factory index eased to 53.2 from 53.9, missing a 54.0 forecast, and manufacturing output dropped to 51.9, a 13-month low. S&P Global says the third-quarter survey data are now consistent with annualized growth approaching 3.0%, up from about 1.5% in the second quarter.

Why it matters: Flash PMIs are survey data, not hard output, and they are revised. Still, a services-led acceleration alongside softening factory output is a specific shape: demand is holding up in the part of the economy where most people work, while goods production cools.

Markets Aug 21, 2026

A Friday bounce did not rescue the week, and the long end stayed stubborn

The Dow closed up 517.80 points, or 0.98%, at 53,277.01. The S&P 500 added 0.43% to 7,674.37 and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.43% to 26,180.46. Weekly, all three finished lower: the S&P 500 off about 1.4%, the Nasdaq off 2.0%, the Dow off 0.8%.

Yields were the week's story. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's expanded bond buyback program, at more than $4 billion per issue, initially pushed yields down before the move reversed; the 10-year finished around 4.73% and the 30-year around 5.27%, both a couple of basis points higher on the day. Quote sources differ by about a basis point at the long end depending on whether you use an intraday print or the official par curve. In corporate news, BJ's Wholesale rose more than 3% on 11.9% same-store sales growth and adjusted EPS of $1.36.

Why it matters: A buyback program that markets judged insufficient means the long end is being set by demand for duration, not by the Fed's policy rate. That is what keeps 30-year mortgage rates and small-business borrowing costs elevated even when the front end is on hold.
The macro backdrop: TSD's internal read has the regime at a late-cycle plateau with the Fed on hold — direction sideways, medium conviction. The nearest alert-grade tripwire is a 10-year close above 4.75%, with the official August 21 par row at 4.74%, one basis point of clearance; the 30-year sits exactly on its 5.25% line. The next dated catalyst is the expanded Treasury buyback operations, effective September 9 through November 4. TSD Macroscope board (as of 2026-08-21).

Milestone Aug 19–20, 2026

Federal debt passed $40 trillion for the first time

Treasury Department data put total US debt at roughly $40.05 trillion, the first time it has crossed $40 trillion. It arrived about four and a half years after the $30 trillion mark and roughly five months after $39 trillion — faster than forecasters had expected. A decade ago the figure was $19.4 trillion.

Part of the acceleration came from lost revenue: the Supreme Court invalidated many of the tariffs imposed under the current administration, and Treasury has been refunding more than $100 billion of import taxes collected under them. Interest on the debt has totaled close to $1.2 trillion so far this year, making it the largest single budget expenditure outside Social Security and Medicare.

Why it matters: Interest costs near $1.2 trillion a year are now a first-order claim on the federal budget, and heavier issuance is one of the forces holding long-term yields up. For anyone borrowing at a rate tied to the long end — a mortgage, an equipment loan, an SBA note — that is the connection between a headline number and a monthly payment.

Prices Aug 21, 2026

Amazon raised prices across Echo, Kindle, Fire TV and eero overnight

Amazon lifted prices across its hardware line without announcement. The base Echo Dot went from $49.99 to $79.99 and the Echo Show 11 from $219.99 to $249.99. The 16GB Kindle rose from $109.99 to $149.99 and the Kindle Paperwhite from $159.99 to $199.99. Fire TV Stick HD went $34.99 to $39.99 and the 4K Max $59.99 to $84.99. The eero 7 mesh system moved from $349.99 to $399.99 and the eero Pro 7 from $699.99 to $799.99. Ring products were not increased.

An Amazon spokeswoman confirmed the increases, saying the consumer electronics industry is facing "significant increases in memory and storage component costs" and that the company adjusted pricing after absorbing them "for as long as we could." Apple raised Mac and iPad prices in June for the same reason. The underlying cause is a memory chip shortage driven by demand for AI compute.

Why it matters: This is the AI buildout showing up on a household receipt. Memory is a shared input across phones, laptops, TVs, cars and appliances, so a shortage driven by data-center demand raises prices on goods that have nothing to do with AI — and it lands in the goods side of inflation data with a lag.

Markets Aug 21, 2026

Bitcoin posted its best week in more than two years

Bitcoin traded near $78,000 on Friday after briefly touching above $79,500, its highest since May, and finished the week up roughly 22–24% — the strongest weekly performance since February 2024. Spot bitcoin ETFs took in about $1.6 billion over the week. The move triggered more than $1 billion of bitcoin short liquidations inside about an hour, with total crypto short liquidations reaching a record $2.7 billion.

Two explanations are competing, and both are worth weighing rather than picking. One reads it as a liquidity story tied to Treasury's decision to double long-bond buybacks. The other points to a regulatory catalyst, with a push in Congress on the Clarity Act. Bloomberg noted thinner weekend liquidity heading into an $80,000 test.

Why it matters: A 22% weekly move with record short liquidations describes a positioning unwind as much as a change in fundamentals, and forced liquidations amplify moves in both directions. For anyone managing their own money, the useful takeaway is the mechanism, not the direction.

◆ Science & space

Launch Aug 20, 2026

SpaceX crossed 11,000 satellites in low Earth orbit

A Falcon 9 lifted 29 Starlink satellites from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station on the Starlink 10-39 mission, after a last-minute scrub the previous day for an undisclosed issue. It was the 75th Starlink mission of 2026, and it carried the constellation past 11,000 satellites in low Earth orbit.

Why it matters: Eleven thousand active satellites is a scale that changes both rural broadband availability and the practical questions around orbital congestion and astronomy. For rural North Carolina in particular, satellite service is increasingly the realistic alternative to waiting on fiber.
