◆ Economy and markets

MarketsOct 19

Wall Street bounces after its worst week since June

The S&P 500 closed up 0.41% at 7,691, the Nasdaq rose about 1.1% to 26,431 and the Dow slipped 0.07% to 51,966. The prior week, the Nasdaq fell 4.5% (its worst since late June) and the S&P 500 fell 1.95%. Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 0.3% early Tuesday, per a market wrap.

Why it matters: One up day does not undo a week like that. If you hold index funds, the number to watch is your own time horizon, and a plan to rebalance on a schedule beats reacting to single sessions.

EnergyOct 20

Brent crude near $110 on Strait of Hormuz tensions

The Oct 20 market wrap cites Brent near $110 and links oil to rate fears. Benzinga's headline the same morning says Iran threatened to target undersea cables, a report I could not open in full, so treat that item as headline-level. Gold was about $4,135 and EUR/USD about 1.1235.

Why it matters: High oil feeds fuel and shipping costs for small businesses that drive or deliver, and it can keep the Fed cautious. Fixed-price contracts signed now carry that cost risk.
