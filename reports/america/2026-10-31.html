◆ Economy and markets

Markets Oct 30

Intel's 28.6% drop leads the Nasdaq down 2.86%, closing its worst month since March 2025

The S&P 500 closed at 7,580.07 (−1.82%), the Dow at 51,770.31 (−1.09%) and the Nasdaq at 25,437 (−2.86%). The Russell 2000 fell 1.66% and the VIX rose about 13% to 19.4. Intel fell after a weak fourth-quarter outlook tied to PC sales; Amazon's negative free cash flow, over $42B for the 12 months through September, added to concern about tech spending.

For the month, the Nasdaq lost about 5.3% and the S&P 500 about 1.2%, while the Dow gained about 1.5%.

Why it matters: For anyone holding index funds, October was a split month: broad and value-heavy funds held up while tech-heavy funds fell. Concentration in a handful of large tech names is the main driver of the gap.

Fed Oct 28–30

Core PCE runs 3.1% and markets keep pricing a December rate hike as more likely than not

September core PCE rose 0.3% on the month and 3.1% from a year earlier; headline PCE reached 3.6%. After the Fed held at 3.75%–4% on Oct 28 with two dissents in favor of a hike, CME FedWatch put December hike odds at about 85%. By Friday's close, one tracker showed about 69%. The 10-year Treasury yield ended near 4.93%.

The Fed's next decision is Dec 9.

Why it matters: Borrowing costs for mortgages, equipment loans and business credit lines are more likely to rise than fall before year-end. Anyone planning to finance a purchase has a reason to lock rates sooner.

Energy Oct 30

Oil holds above $100 while gold drops 3.3%

WTI crude settled near $104 and Brent around $112–115. Exxon Mobil and Chevron rose on wider refining margins. Gold futures fell 3.3% to about $3,899.

Why it matters: Fuel and delivery costs stay high for households and for businesses that run vans or trucks, including most trades. Expect fuel surcharges to stick through the holidays.

◆ Money, work and safety

Crypto Oct 29–30

Binance estimates a $6.3 billion theft; Binance.US says it was not affected

The exchange paused deposits and withdrawals after what may be the largest crypto theft on record. About $3B has been frozen with help from stablecoin issuers. Binance says it will cover the losses. Bitcoin traded near $77,000 on Friday.

Why it matters: Americans holding crypto on the global Binance platform may face delays getting funds out. It's a reminder that assets held on an exchange depend on that exchange's security.
NPR CNBC
Jobs Oct 30

AT&T and Verizon cut about 8,000 jobs in the third quarter

Light Reading counts roughly 8,000 positions eliminated at the two carriers in Q3 and argues AI is not the main cause. Separately, an SHL survey of more than 1,000 U.S. workers found nearly one in five people laid off or replaced by AI were later asked to come back.

Why it matters: Telecom employment keeps shrinking. The October jobs report on Nov 6 is the next broad read on hiring.
