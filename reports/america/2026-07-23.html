◆ Markets & the economy

Markets Jul 22, 2026

Stocks slip as Alphabet and Tesla open Big Tech earnings season

The S&P 500 slipped 0.13% to 7,499, the Nasdaq fell 0.57% to 25,691, and the Dow was essentially flat at 52,219, with rising oil prices weighing on growth names. Alphabet and Tesla were the first two of the "Magnificent Seven" to report Q2 results; Alphabet's stock fell after hours despite a strong quarter as investors reacted to its capital-spending growth. Elsewhere, Super Micro Computer jumped 26% on a record backlog and AT&T rose about 4% after beating EPS estimates.

Why it matters: The market's swing factor right now is how much the big platforms are spending on AI infrastructure and whether that spending pays off. For anyone managing their own money, "strong quarter, stock down on capex" is the tell — investors are scrutinizing the return on the AI build-out, not just headline earnings.
Motley Fool ◆ TheStreet
Fed Jul 2026

Inflation at 4.2%; Fed holds and drops forward guidance

The all-items CPI rose 4.2% over the year to May 2026, the largest 12-month increase since April 2023, and 2026 headline inflation is tracking about a point higher than expected a year ago. The Fed is holding its target at 3.50–3.75%; after the June meeting, Chair Kevin Warsh shifted communications away from traditional forward guidance toward pure data dependence. Markets are now pricing in at least one 25-basis-point rate increase in 2026, with FOMC participants forecasting 2.2% GDP growth for the year.

Why it matters: Sticky inflation plus a data-dependent Fed means rates stay higher for longer and small-business borrowing stays expensive. Budgets tighten, so buyers scrutinize ROI harder — services that pay for themselves quickly have the easier sell.

Wildfire Jul 22, 2026

Western wildfires pull in military air support

An interagency situation report for July 22 documented active large fires across the West, including one about 13 miles northeast of Sisters, Oregon exhibiting extreme fire behavior — torching, running and spotting — with structures and critical infrastructure threatened and evacuations in effect. Military air support was deployed, including an air wing to San Bernardino, California and a C-130 airtanker with personnel to Santa Maria, California.

Why it matters: Peak Western fire season is straining firefighting resources enough to draw in military aircraft, with direct effects on air quality, evacuations and insurance costs across affected states.
NIFC — Incident Management Situation Report
Charlotte / NC Jul 2026

Charlotte lands SMBC's second US HQ; NC slips to #2 for business

Japanese bank SMBC Group selected Charlotte for its second US headquarters, bringing roughly 2,000 jobs and a $50.5M investment. Regional leaders say the metro has announced 15 new projects totaling over 4,500 jobs since July 1, with more than $1.4B in potential investment on the table. North Carolina slipped from #1 to #2 on CNBC's state-for-business ranking on affordability, crime and worker-protection scores, though its economy still ranked #1 nationally.

Why it matters: Charlotte's growth engine keeps drawing major employers and thousands of jobs, which feeds demand for local services and small-business formation across the metro. The affordability slip is the flip side of that growth.
WCNC ◆ Axios Charlotte
Space Jul 2026

SpaceX launches a robotic servicing satellite; NASA backs 41 lunar projects

SpaceX launched a geosynchronous robotic servicing satellite on July 21 for a decade-long mission, following a July 16 post-ignition abort of Starship Flight 13. NASA selected 41 commercial technology projects to tackle challenges for future Moon and Mars missions, from powering lunar outposts to protecting spacecraft from lunar dust.

Why it matters: In-orbit servicing and a widening commercial-technology pipeline point to a maturing space economy where private companies increasingly handle both launch and long-duration operations.
Spaceflight Now — July 2026
