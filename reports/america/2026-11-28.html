◆ Economy and markets

Markets Nov 27 close

Stocks close sharply higher in the half session and log their best week since April 2025

In a session that ended at 1 p.m. ET, the S&P 500 rose 1.60% to 7,297.49, the Dow gained 1.18% to 50,774.94 and the Nasdaq Composite added 2.31% to 24,129.50. Coverage put the S&P's weekly gain near 5.6%, its best since April 2025, with the Nasdaq up about 6.7%. Chip and AI shares led, all 11 S&P sectors rose and SanDisk gained about 16%. Volume was thin, as it usually is the day after Thanksgiving. These figures come from secondary market recaps, so check exchange data before relying on the decimals.

Why it matters: For someone managing their own money, one strong holiday week on light volume reverses part of a volatile month and does not settle where markets are heading. The Fed decision on Dec 9 is the next real test, and markets still price a hike.

Retail Nov 27

Black Friday online sales hit $9.7 billion by evening and head for a record

Adobe counted $9.7 billion in US online spending through 6:30 p.m. ET Friday, up 12.8% from the same point last year. It now projects $13.0 to $13.4 billion for the full day, above its September forecast of $12.9 billion and last year's $11.8 billion. Thanksgiving Day itself drew $7.1 billion, up 10.4%. Salesforce, which measures differently, projects $18.7 billion in US online sales, up 4%. Adobe forecasts $15.1 billion for Cyber Monday. These are nominal dollars, and inflation accounts for part of the growth.

Why it matters: Consumers are still spending online despite high prices, and the rest of the season depends on Cyber Monday. Adobe's final Black Friday count usually comes out today.

◆ Travel, weather and North Carolina

Weather Forecast Nov 27–28

A Sunday storm threatens the busiest return-travel day

Saturday looks like a decent travel day for most of the country, with snow in the northern and central Rockies. A system forming over the Rockies and Plains late Saturday is forecast to bring rain, a few thunderstorms and gusts near 60 mph from the Mississippi Valley to the East Coast on Sunday. Forecasters flagged possible delays at O'Hare, Cincinnati, Atlanta and Denver. TSA expects more than 3 million people at checkpoints on the peak day. Rain reaches the Appalachians and the Atlantic coast Sunday night into Monday.

Why it matters: Drivers and flyers heading home Sunday, including through Charlotte, should check the National Weather Service and their airline before leaving. Leaving Saturday or after Monday morning avoids the worst of it.
CNN weather.com FOX Weather
NC Ends Nov 30

North Carolina's 41-cent gas tax returns after the suspension lapses Nov 30

The legislature suspended the state's 41-cents-per-gallon motor fuel tax through Nov 30 in a bill Gov. Josh Stein signed Oct 9. Legislative leaders said on Nov 17 that they will not extend the suspension, so the tax resumes next month. Economists estimated drivers saw 30 to 35 cents of the cut at the pump, and AAA put the state average at $3.08 at the time of the WFAE report.

Why it matters: North Carolina drivers and businesses that run vehicles should expect a pump-price increase of roughly 30 to 40 cents a gallon starting in early December. Filling up before the end of Monday saves a little.
