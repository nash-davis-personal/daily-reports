◆ Hawaii

Developing Aug 15–16, 2026

Hurricane Lala lashes the Big Island; one dead, most of the island without power

Lala made its closest approach to Hawaii's Big Island late Saturday evening at hurricane strength, then weakened Sunday morning to a tropical storm with 70 mph sustained winds. One person is dead and tens of thousands have lost power, with roughly 70% of the Big Island out as of Sunday.

Rainfall drove the worst of it. Windward totals over the preceding 36 hours passed one to two feet across most of the windward side, with isolated stations reporting as much as 35 inches. A flash flood emergency was issued for parts of the island for ongoing life-threatening flooding. Local law enforcement reported two homes swept away at Naalehu, and Hawaii County Mayor Kimo Alameda said several occupied homes may have been swept off their foundations in the Alihu and Waiheno areas, with search and rescue underway. The system is now carrying its impacts westward across Maui toward Honolulu and Kauai.

Why it matters: Hawaii's power grid is island-by-island with no mainland interconnection, so a single storm track can take most of an island's customers offline at once and restoration depends on crews and parts already on the island. Anyone with travel, family or business exposure across Maui, Oahu or Kauai should expect impacts to continue into the start of the week.
NBC News · CNN · Hawaii News Now · National Hurricane Center

◆ The Midwest flood

Developing Aug 14–16, 2026

White River at Anderson crests at a record 24.9 feet, beating a mark from 1913

A sustained run of thunderstorms dropped more than 11 inches of rain in parts of the Midwest over the past week, with Indiana and Ohio hit hardest. The White River at Anderson, Indiana rose to a record 24.9 feet, surpassing a flood mark that had stood since 1913. The National Weather Service said Indianapolis was facing a flood it had not seen in more than 30 years, with the potential to approach a 100-year event.

The river peaked on the north side of Marion County Saturday night. Officials had urged residents of Broad Ripple, Ravenswood and Rocky Ripple to evacuate Friday afternoon, and Mayor Joe Hogsett declared an emergency. At least three people have died — reporting links five deaths to the wider storm system — and more than 150,000 customers were without power as of Saturday afternoon. Indiana is seeking federal aid. Flash flood and flood warnings ran across central Illinois and central Indiana, with watches from Iowa and Missouri east to central West Virginia, and more heavy rain expected through Sunday.

Why it matters: Record river crests reset the maps that insurers, lenders and local governments use to decide what counts as a flood-risk property. When a gauge breaks a 113-year record, the practical consequences — insurance rates, building requirements, mortgage conditions — show up over the following year for people who were never told they lived in a flood zone.
NPR · CBS News · WFYI live updates · WISH-TV

◆ Fire season

Ongoing Aug 15–16, 2026

National preparedness level 5, with 88 uncontained large fires and 7.2 million acres burned

The National Interagency Fire Center's August 15 situation report lists the national preparedness level at 5, the top of the scale, with 88 uncontained large fires being worked, 125 new fires reported that day, 29,363 personnel assigned nationwide, and 7,202,974 acres burned year to date.

In Oregon, the Austin Fire in the Mount Hood National Forest southeast of Estacada grew past 2,000 acres and crossed the Clackamas River, still at 0% containment Saturday morning. The High Lava Fire in the Gifford Pinchot National Forest in Skamania County, Washington reached 1,214 acres at 8% contained. Oregon Highway 35 between US 26 and Parkdale remains closed indefinitely for firefighting. In Colorado, the Aspen Acres Fire stands at 102,004 acres and 72% contained, and the Gold Mountain Fire at 39,691 acres and 87% contained.

Why it matters: Preparedness level 5 means national firefighting resources are committed and competing, so new starts get slower initial attack. For households in or near the affected corridors, that raises the value of having evacuation plans and documents ready before a warning arrives rather than after.
NIFC national situation report (Aug 15) · KPTV · OPB

◆ Markets and money

Markets Aug 16, 2026

Bond investors are focused on $70 billion of off-balance-sheet backstops behind AI data centers

Roughly $70 billion in residual-value guarantees tied to AI infrastructure sits off the balance sheets of the chipmakers providing them. The structure lets a supplier backstop a customer's debt — agreeing to cover a shortfall if the chips do not hold their book value at liquidation — without recording the liability. Nvidia's financing partnership reportedly involves residual-value support on up to 25% of certain projects.

Moody's and S&P Global have warned that growing contingent obligations could constrain chipmakers' financial flexibility. Analysts at CreditSights described the structure as writing a put that is "pro-cyclical," near-costless while demand is strong and most relevant in a sharp downturn. Separately, tech companies have borrowed roughly $220 billion in 2026 for infrastructure, and AMD priced a $4.75 billion bond sale on August 13.

Why it matters: For anyone managing their own money with AI-linked exposure, this is a reminder that a chunk of the buildout's risk is not visible in the headline financials of the companies carrying it. It is not evidence of trouble. It is a reason to read the contingent-liability footnotes rather than only the revenue line.
Business Standard / Bloomberg (Aug 16) · Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance

◆ Science and space

Record Aug 15, 2026

SpaceX set a new turnaround record with two Falcon 9 launches 38.5 minutes apart

A Falcon 9 lifted off from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station at 9:12 p.m. EDT Saturday carrying eight satellites for Globalstar, a Louisiana telecom company; all eight deployed over about six minutes beginning 56.5 minutes after liftoff. At 9:50 p.m. EDT, a second Falcon 9 launched the USSF-366 mission for the US Space Force from Vandenberg Space Force Base in California — 38.5 minutes after the first, the shortest gap between two SpaceX launches to date.

Why it matters: Launch cadence is the constraint on satellite communications capacity, which is what carries rural broadband, emergency connectivity and GPS augmentation. A shorter turnaround between missions means constellation replenishment gets cheaper and faster, which shows up eventually as coverage in places terrestrial networks skip.
Space.com · Spaceflight Now
