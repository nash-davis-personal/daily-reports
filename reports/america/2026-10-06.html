◆ Economy

RatesOct. 5

Bond yields ignore the payroll miss

September payrolls rose 29,000 against an 84,000 forecast, unemployment moved to 4.2% and prior months were revised down 60,000. Odds of an October Fed rate increase fell to roughly 23%, but the 10-year Treasury still sat above 5.28% and closed Monday at 5.31%, a 52-week high, while the S&P 500 gained about 0.7% and the Nasdaq closed at a record. Vice Chair Jefferson said the committee may wait for more data; Dallas Fed President Logan said at least another 50 basis points of tightening may be needed.

Why it matters: Borrowing stays expensive for households and small businesses even if the Fed pauses, because long-term rates are set by the bond market. Cash savers still earn high yields; variable-rate debt stays costly.

Brent held near $102 a barrel with WTI near $90, a wide gap that reflects Middle East supply concerns and shipping costs, per one briefing. Yesterday's edition reported record diesel at $6.505 a gallon.
