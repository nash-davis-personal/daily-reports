◆ Small business and national

Policy Oct 1

SBA 7(a) acquisition-loan rules tighten

Under SOP 50 10 8.1, the minimum debt-service coverage ratio rises from 1.15x to 1.25x for many initial acquisitions, every SBA-financed change of ownership needs an independent valuation, and deals of $3 million or more may need a Quality of Earnings report. Separately, the SBA's proposed rewrite of small-business size standards has a comment period through Nov 20. Sources are law-firm and advisor blogs; check the SOP text.

Why it matters: Buyers of small businesses face more paperwork and a higher bar for loan approval, which can slow deals.
