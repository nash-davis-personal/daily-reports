◆ Economy and markets

JobsOct 2

September hiring nearly stalls at 29,000

Nonfarm payrolls rose 29,000 in September against an expected 90,000, after 133,000 in August. July and August were revised down by 60,000 combined, so the net change including revisions was a loss of 31,000. Unemployment rose to 4.2% from 4.1%. Healthcare added 23,000 and construction 11,000; information fell 10,000 and government fell 17,000. Average hourly earnings rose 0.1% on the month and 3.0% on the year.

Why it matters: Slower hiring and slower wage growth point to a softer labor market. For a small business owner it can mean easier hiring and tighter customer spending.

◆ Weather and disasters

StormOct 9–10

Hurricane Isaias makes landfall near Destin, Florida

Isaias came ashore as a Category 2 hurricane with 105 mph sustained winds, bringing damaging wind, heavy rain, flooding and storm surge to the Florida Panhandle and southern Alabama. More than 664,000 customers lost power across Florida and Alabama, at least four people died, and Alabama deployed about 5,000 National Guard members. The storm weakened after landfall.

Why it matters: Power restoration and cleanup in the Florida Panhandle and southern Alabama set the pace for local businesses reopening.
