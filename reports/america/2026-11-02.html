◆ Energy and markets

Energy Nov 1

OPEC+ keeps December output targets unchanged

Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Algeria, Kazakhstan and Oman agreed in a Sunday video call to hold December quotas where they are, matching what Reuters sources reported Friday. Bloomberg called the decision "largely symbolic" because the Iran war is still keeping much of the Gulf's output offline; most Gulf producers pump below their targets. A full ministerial meeting on Nov 29 will set policy for 2027, including whether to restore another layer of cuts made in 2022.

Why it matters: No new supply was added, so the price of fuel depends on the conflict more than on OPEC+ for now. The Nov 29 meeting is the next scheduled supply decision.
Rigzone / Bloomberg (Nov 2) · Bloomberg (Nov 1)
Markets Nov 2, early

Oil falls more than 4% and stock futures rise on reports of US-Iran diplomatic talks

By about 6:25 a.m. ET, Brent was down 4.5% at $108.38 and WTI down more than 4% at $99.31, after reports that the US and Iran are exploring diplomatic options to end the conflict. Crude had risen more than 10% in October. S&P 500 futures were up 0.5%, Nasdaq-100 futures 0.8% and Dow futures 310 points. October ended with the Nasdaq's worst month since March 2025, while the Dow rose nearly 2%.

Sources disagree on Gulf shipping. Bloomberg reports crude shipments through the Strait of Hormuz back near prewar levels; the IMF's PortWatch data counted only 2 to 5 transits a day from Oct 19 to Oct 25, according to the TSD Macroscope board. Both are shown here because they point in different directions.

Why it matters: If the drop holds, it should show up at the pump within a week or two, which helps households and any business that drives for a living. Early-morning moves can reverse by the close, especially on diplomacy headlines.

Week ahead Nov 2 – Nov 6

Earnings tonight, Election Day Tuesday, the refunding Wednesday and the jobs report Friday

Apple and Palantir report after the close today; AMD reports Tuesday and Qualcomm Wednesday. Midterm elections are Tuesday, Nov 3, and some results may take days to finalize. The Treasury's quarterly refunding announcement is Wednesday, Nov 4, and the October Employment Situation report comes Friday, Nov 6 at 8:30 a.m. ET, with economists expecting about +55,000 jobs after September's +29,000. ISM's October manufacturing survey is also due at the start of the week; calendars list it for either Monday or Tuesday.

Why it matters: The jobs number is the last major labor reading before the Fed's December meeting. Anyone with a variable-rate loan or credit line will feel that decision directly.
