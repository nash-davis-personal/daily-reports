◆ Markets & the economy

New Jul 10, 2026

SK Hynix raises $26.5B in the largest foreign IPO in US history

The South Korean memory-chip maker priced 177.9 million shares at $149 and jumped as much as 17% in its Nasdaq debut on Friday, July 10, raising $26.5 billion. That tops Alibaba's 2014 offering as the biggest US listing ever by a non-American company. Regular trading under the ticker SKHY begins Monday, July 13.

SK Hynix is a primary supplier of the high-bandwidth memory that goes into AI accelerators, so the debut gives US investors direct access to a core AI-hardware name for the first time. It landed in the same week chip stocks had sold off on doubts about AI capital spending, then rebounded into Friday.

Why it matters: A record raise means a major AI-supply-chain company is now directly investable on a US exchange, and its reception is a real-time read on how much appetite the market still has for AI-hardware exposure.
TechCrunch — SK Hynix's record US IPO
New Jul 10, 2026

Stocks close higher Friday; S&P 500 and Nasdaq post weekly wins

The S&P 500 rose 0.42% to 7,575.39, the Nasdaq gained 0.29% to 26,281.61, and the Dow added 0.29% to 52,637.01 on Friday, July 10, capping a choppy but net-positive week for the two big indexes. A rebound in chip stocks led the move after a mid-week decline driven by questions about whether AI infrastructure spending will pay off.

Why it matters: A weekly gain near record index levels means most retirement and brokerage balances ended the week up, despite the mid-week volatility that dominated the headlines.
Yahoo Finance — Markets, Friday Jul 10
Update Jul 10, 2026

Fuel prices swing as a Strait of Hormuz risk premium holds

US average gasoline fell to $2.98 a gallon on Friday, down about 2% on the day, even as crude held a weekly gain of roughly 3.5%. The premium in oil is tied to disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz — a waterway that carries about 20% of world oil and gas trade — amid renewed US–Iran tensions. The UAE raised crude output to a record to help offset the disruption.

Why it matters: Pump prices are still roughly a third higher than a year ago, a direct cost for households and for any small business that runs vehicles or takes deliveries.

◆ Weather & the states

New Jul 10, 2026

Flash-flood emergency swamps southeast Missouri

The National Weather Service declared a Flash Flood Emergency early Friday, July 10, for parts of Iron and Reynolds counties in southeast Missouri after slow-moving storms dropped 6 to 11 inches of rain, warning of a "catastrophic" damage threat. The Black River near Annapolis crested at 16.1 feet, more than double its 8-foot flood stage, forcing water rescues and road closures. The excessive-rain risk extended into Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky and Tennessee.

Why it matters: Life-threatening flooding across a multi-state area drove emergency rescues and closures, with more heavy rain in the forecast for the mid-South.
The Watchers — SE Missouri flash-flood emergency
New Jul 10, 2026

North Carolina ends a year-long budget fight amid heat and drought

Gov. Josh Stein signed a $34 billion North Carolina state budget on July 10, ending roughly a year of negotiations that had stalled state spending. The same week, a heat wave pushed highs near 100°F with feels-like readings up to 110°F, and much of Orange County stayed in exceptional drought — the most severe category — straining reservoirs across central North Carolina.

Why it matters: The budget unlocks a year of delayed state spending, while the drought is prompting water restrictions across the Charlotte–Triangle region — a local operating condition for any NC business.
WUNC — NC news roundup, Jul 10
