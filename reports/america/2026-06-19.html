◆ The economy

Markets Jun 17

Fed holds at 3.50%–3.75% but signals a possible hike

The Federal Reserve held its benchmark rate steady for a fourth straight meeting in a unanimous 12–0 vote — the first decision under new Chair Kevin Warsh. The bigger news was the projections: the Fed raised its year-end PCE inflation forecast to 3.6% (from 2.7% in March) and core PCE to 3.3%, and 17 of 18 officials judged inflation risks tilted to the upside. The median policymaker now expects rates to end 2026 higher than today, a flip from March, when the median still implied a cut. Warsh declined to share a personal forecast, shortened the official statement, removed forward guidance, and announced task forces to review Fed operations.

Why it matters: "Higher for longer" got more concrete. For anyone managing their own money, the path to rate cuts narrowed and short-term yields stay attractive; for small businesses, borrowing costs aren't easing soon, so pricing discipline and cost control carry weight through the rest of the year.

Local Jun 2026

Charlotte lands an SMBC headquarters and an AssetMark hub

Japanese bank SMBC Group selected Charlotte for its second U.S. headquarters — about 2,000 jobs and a $50.5 million investment. Separately, wealth-management platform AssetMark Financial will invest $10 million to open an East Coast hub in Charlotte's central business district, creating 252 jobs. Charlotte's unemployment rate sits at 3.6%, and North Carolina continues to run below the national rate as the broader labor market cools.

Why it matters: Charlotte keeps deepening as a finance hub, which pulls in vendors, contractors, and the small businesses that serve a growing professional workforce — a healthy demand backdrop for local service providers.

NASA Jun 2026

ISS air leak worsens; engineers weigh a riskier repair

NASA says a long-running air leak aboard the International Space Station recently got worse, prompting engineers to investigate new suspected crack locations and consider a more aggressive repair strategy. The leak has been tracked for years; the recent change moved it up the priority list.

Why it matters: A concrete reminder of the aging-hardware problem on the ISS as NASA plans its transition to commercial stations later this decade.

Research Jun 2026

A possible new trigger for Alzheimer's

Scientists report evidence that amyloid beta may interfere with tau — a protein that keeps neurons functioning — rather than plaques themselves being the root cause of Alzheimer's. If it holds up, it points research and potential treatments at a different mechanism than the long-dominant plaque hypothesis.

Why it matters: Alzheimer's affects millions of American families; a shift in the leading hypothesis could redirect how the disease is studied and treated.
