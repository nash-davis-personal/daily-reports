◆ Models and pricing

Pricing Oct 13 GA

Gemini 4 Argon is cheap until Nov 30, then costs double

Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available on October 13 with a 1M-token context. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, with cached input 95% off, through November 30. After that it is $4 / $20. A secondary roundup (DEV Community) cites Simon Willison saying the post-promo price puts Argon at parity with Claude Opus 5.5; that comparison is his claim and I did not verify it.

No parameter count or benchmark table appeared in the sources I could reach, so treat capability claims as Google's.

So what for TSD: If any client workload runs on a metered model, Argon is worth a benchmark run this month, and the price you measure is the promo price. Quote clients on the $4 / $20 figure so a December invoice does not surprise them.
DEV Community roundup Argon enterprise write-up
Open weights Oct 15–16

Meta and Reflection ship 500B-plus open-weight MoE models

Meta released Muse Spark Open on October 15: roughly 816B total parameters with about 42B active, up to 1M tokens of context, multimodal input and text output, pitched at agentic coding. License terms were not stated in the roundup. Reflection released Beam on October 16: 501B total, 23B active, 1M context, Apache 2.0, with a ~280GB NVFP4 checkpoint that runs on four B200 GPUs and day-zero support in transformers, vLLM and SGLang.

Vercel AI Gateway already lists Beam, free during launch (covered in the Oct 17 Technology edition).

So what for TSD: Nobody runs these in-house. The value is price pressure on hosted inference and a credible Apache-licensed option for clients who insist their data never reaches a closed vendor. Try Beam through the Gateway while it is free.

◆ Infrastructure and agents

Compute Oct 16–17

Anthropic reportedly signs its first Canadian data-center lease

Reuters (syndicated) reports Anthropic signed a 20-year lease with EverWind Fuels at Point Tupper, Nova Scotia: phase one is 95 MW, targeted for late 2027, with the campus planned to reach 420 MW by 2028. EverWind's own announcement of a C$10B project (C$8B campus, C$2B onshore wind) described the tenant only as an unnamed frontier AI developer, so the Anthropic name rests on Reuters.

The wind farms will not be ready by the time the first phase opens.

So what for TSD: No action. It is another data point that capacity is the constraint the labs are buying their way out of, which is the same story as the grid and permit fights in recent editions.
Reuters via The Star CP24 / Canadian Press
Agents Oct 18

A Claude-linked scraper gets an Instagram account suspended by bot detection

Meta spokesperson Andy Stone said a Claude-linked scraping tool acted on an advocacy group's Instagram account at about four times normal human speed, which triggered bot detection and a suspension of about 90 minutes. I saw this through The Next Web via an aggregator feed and did not confirm it with Meta directly.

So what for TSD: Any agent you build that drives a logged-in third-party site needs human-paced rate limits and a way to hand off to a person. Put that in the spec for booking-automation work before a client's account is the one that gets flagged.
