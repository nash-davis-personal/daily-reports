◆ Platforms open up

Platform Nov 3, 2026

Apple opens Siri AI to every developer in the iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS 27.3 betas

Apple's developer post says a new Developer Preview lets any developer integrate their app with Siri AI starting with the 27.3 betas. Apps expose content and actions through App Intents, a new "skills" mechanism lets Siri chain several actions into one task, and any entity in the Spotlight semantic index becomes reachable. Fifteen Apple apps already use it. App Store submission opens in the spring and the customer rollout is "in a few months." It requires an Apple Intelligence device in one of six supported languages, is unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, and server-side features have daily usage limits with expanded access "a paid option in the future."

The same day, Apple's annual 10-K added risk language saying AI agents "may behave in unintended or unpredictable ways," can take actions not authorized by the user, and that Apple's ability to oversee third-party agents may be limited.

So what for TSD: Any client app or wrapped PWA can become a Siri action with a handful of App Intents ("book my next detail," "what's my order status"). The skills model mirrors how Claude skills work, so intent design transfers. Nothing ships to customers until next year, so this is a line in mobile upsell proposals for now.

Data Nov 3, 2026

OpenAI says ChatGPT's search volume is about 20% of Google's

At a New York press event, ChatGPT consumer product lead Adam Fry said ChatGPT's search queries are "roughly one-fifth" of Google's. OpenAI counts a search whenever a prompt makes ChatGPT hit the web, and did not say which Google figure it used. Ahrefs estimated 12% in February under a narrower definition. Fry also said most users do not seem to mind ads in ChatGPT and suggested OpenAI could show more.

Related: Google announced Gemini will be off by default in Google Classroom, and that districts which turn off AI on school devices also lose Google Search, because "Search and AI are deeply integrated." Testing starts this month with a small group of US schools; broad rollout is early 2027.

So what for TSD: Two vendors said the same thing in different words: search and AI answers are one product now. Add an "AI search visibility" line to every local SEO engagement: structured data, a clean FAQ page, and a check of what ChatGPT and Gemini say when someone asks for a plumber in Gastonia. It is cheap to deliver and easy for Grant and Bishop to explain.

◆ Agents: law, security and provenance

Law Nov 3, 2026

EU advocate general: training is covered by the TDM exception, but a chatbot's retrieval-time output can infringe

In Like Company v Google, Advocate General Maciej Szpunar told the Court of Justice of the EU that training large language models on protected works is generally allowed under the text-and-data-mining exception, and recommended the court not rule on training because the evidence did not show the publisher's articles were used. He said infringement may arise when a chatbot circumvents paywalls to fetch articles, or when outputs reproduce recognizable protected expression. Gemini uses retrieval-augmented generation. The opinion is advisory, and the court usually follows its advocates general.

So what for TSD: This is the first high-court signal that the retrieval step, which is exactly what TSD Concierge does, carries more exposure than training. The fix is already TSD's default: index only content the client owns or licenses (their site, their docs, their menus), never third-party articles. Write that into the Concierge spec as a stated rule so it reads as a feature.

Security Nov 3, 2026

CrowdStrike buys Fiddler AI and Dreadnode and ships an "AI Kill Switch" for rogue agents

At Fal.Con Europe, CrowdStrike announced agreements to acquire Fiddler AI (agent observability and explainability) and Dreadnode (automated AI attack testing with more than 100 methods mapped to the OWASP LLM Top 10 and NIST AI RMF). Prices were undisclosed; the Wall Street Journal reported about $400M and $95M. Fiddler becomes "Falcon Observe," with general availability planned for CrowdStrike's fiscal Q4 2027. CEO George Kurtz also unveiled an AI Kill Switch inside Falcon Guardian that can terminate a misbehaving agent, block restarts and cut its network access, available now, and said CrowdStrike is working with Anthropic to protect Claude Cowork. Kurtz: "Everyone is building AI agents, but they never had the off button, and now they do."

Also Nov 3: OpenAI said it is partnering with Cathedral, the firm founded by former DOGE staff that got into Anthropic's internal systems through a bug-bounty engagement last week, and is "more than quadrupling" rewards for critical findings. Anthropic said model weights and Claude conversations were untouched.

So what for TSD: "Can you turn it off?" is now a question SMB owners will have heard. Build the answer into TSD Front Desk and Concierge: an audit log of every action the agent took, a per-client kill switch in the dashboard, and a one-page "what the agent can and cannot do" sheet. It costs a day and closes deals.

Policy Effective Nov 3, 2026 (reported)

Anthropic's invisible text watermark lands on four older Claude models, with no opt-out

Per an email to API customers reported by The Decoder on Oct 31 and echoed in developer posts dated Nov 3, Anthropic is retrofitting its model-level text watermark to Mythos 5, Opus 4.7, Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 to meet the EU AI Act's December deadline; models released after Aug 2, 2026 already carry it. It cannot be switched off. On Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 only, the mark applies when temperature, top_p and top_k stay at defaults; changing any one produces unmarked output, and Anthropic's email tells anyone relying on the watermark for compliance to leave those settings alone. Detection runs through a private-preview API for regulators, law enforcement, media and researchers. The Nov 3 effective date comes from secondary posts; we could not fetch an Anthropic page confirming it.

So what for TSD: Any client email, SMS or web copy that comes straight out of these models is now marked. That is fine for an AI receptionist, and worth disclosing in writing for marketing copy. Check which model IDs each client pipeline pins and whether anything touches sampling parameters, then note it in the client's config.

◆ Money and compute

Funding Nov 3, 2026

fal raises $1.3B at a $16B valuation, and says it had to rate-limit customers this summer

fal, the inference host for image, video and audio generation models, raised a $1.3B Series F led by Bessemer with Nvidia, Sound Ventures, HOF Capital and T. Rowe Price, doubling its $8B April valuation. Combined 2026 fundraising is $1.65B. Annualized revenue passed $800M earlier this year with 185 employees. CEO Burkay Gur: "We hit a point in summer where we were not able to serve our customers and had to rate-limit them." Fireworks AI closed at $17.5B in July for comparison.

So what for TSD: TSD Marketing's ad videos run on this layer of the stack. Capacity has been the constraint at the market leader, so keep a second provider wired for image and video generation and quote turnaround times with a buffer.
TechCrunch ◆ SiliconANGLE
Cost Nov 3, 2026

Revolut cut AI coding inference costs about 90% by self-hosting open GLM models

Per The Register's report on an AI Engineer NYC talk, Revolut's David Abitbol said self-hosting GLM 5.1 at about $0.30 per million tokens cost roughly a tenth of Codex or Claude Code for its 2,000 engineers. The company moved from Gemini in early 2025 to Claude Code in early 2026, then to GLM-5.1 and GLM-5.3, which runs on 56 Nvidia B200 GPUs. It reports about 200 tokens per second, sub-3-second time to first token at 256K context, 60% faster coding time, and routes vision queries to Gemini or GPT because GLM is text-only. Abitbol's line: "Long context is a scam."

So what for TSD: $0.30 per million tokens is the price floor to keep in mind when quoting AI features to clients. For a receptionist or concierge handling a few thousand conversations a month, the model bill is a rounding error, so price on outcomes (calls answered, bookings made) and keep the hosted Claude quality for the customer-facing voice.
