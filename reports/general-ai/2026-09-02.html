◆ Anthropic shipped 5.1

New Sept 1, 2026

Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, with the cost moved off the sticker

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on September 1. Input stays at $10 per million tokens and output at $50, unchanged from Fable 5. The change is in cache reads, which dropped to $0.25 per million, a 75% cut. Coverage of the launch puts the practical effect at about 25% cheaper for ordinary workloads and as much as 45% cheaper for agentic ones, which is where a long system prompt and a growing tool history get re-read on every turn.

The capability claims are concentrated in agentic evaluation. Fable 5.1 scores 52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, a scientific research benchmark run in a terminal, against 24.7% for Fable 5. Terminal-Bench 4.0 comes in at 55.8%. The model carries a 1M-token context window with multimodal input and is available through the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry. Mythos 5.1 is the same underlying model at a higher safeguard level, available only to vetted cyberdefense and life-science organizations.

So what for TSD: The RAG concierge and the phone receptionist both re-read a fixed knowledge prompt on every single turn, which is exactly the workload the cache read cut targets. Re-run your per-conversation cost math on the concierge this week with $0.25/M cache reads; if it lands where the vendor claims, the concierge gets cheaper to serve without touching a line of code, and that margin is either your price cut in a competitive bid or your gross margin.

Feature Sept 1, 2026

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards makes zero data retention the default answer

Anthropic announced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards the same day, developed with more than 100 customers across financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, telecom, law, retail and the public sector, plus Amazon, Google and Microsoft. Customer data sits in cloud infrastructure the customer controls rather than Anthropic's, businesses set how data is reviewed and stored, and safety monitoring runs automatically with no Anthropic human review. Anthropic says it will not charge for it and that it works whether a customer goes direct or through a cloud provider.

It covers Claude Code, Claude Enterprise, the Claude Platform, Amazon Bedrock, Google's Agent Platform and Microsoft Foundry. Rollout is phased, beginning later this fall. CNBC framed it as a retention-policy reversal following customer pushback.

So what for TSD: Every SMB prospect who has stalled on "where does my customer data go" now has a vendor answer you can point at instead of arguing. Add one line to the receptionist and concierge proposals citing Anthropic's zero-retention posture, and stop treating data handling as the objection you improvise around on the call.

◆ Both labs spent September 1 on safeguards

Breaking Sept 1, 2026

OpenAI says Astra crosses the Critical cyber threshold

CNBC reported on September 1 that OpenAI says its Astra model crosses the Critical cyber capability threshold defined in its Preparedness Framework. That hardens the position OpenAI took on August 7, when it said it could not rule the capability out and slowed the model's development. The framework defines Critical cyber as a tool-augmented model able to develop functional zero-day exploits across many hardened real-world systems without a human guiding each step, or to devise and execute novel end-to-end attack strategies from a high-level goal.

OpenAI has described pausing internal Astra activity that lacks safeguards, running universal monitoring of the model, and working with government agencies and AI safety organizations on testing. Advanced cyber capabilities are being routed to a vetted set of organizations in a coalition OpenAI calls Daybreak rather than shipped broadly.

So what for TSD: Treat this as a schedule item, not a headline. If frontier models can now find and weaponize unknown flaws at scale, the window between a CVE landing and a bot hitting your client sites gets shorter. Set a recurring Monday check on Vercel and Next.js advisories for every site you host, and make patch turnaround a line in your retainer rather than a favor you do quietly.

◆ Compute and copyright

New Aug 31, 2026

Anthropic committed $35 billion to Lambda for Texas capacity

Bloomberg and the WSJ reported Anthropic signed a $35 billion computing deal on Monday, August 31, with Lambda, an Nvidia-backed cloud provider, for GPU capacity at a data center in Nueces County, Texas. The site covers roughly 350 megawatts and is being developed by Hut 8, a former crypto miner now building AI data centers. Nvidia occupies three positions in the same deal: it supplies the GPUs, it has invested in Lambda, and per a person familiar with the arrangement it holds the lease on the facility.

So what for TSD: Anthropic is buying capacity in units of tens of billions, which is the supply side of the cache-read price cut above. Read it as a signal that per-token costs keep falling and price your AI products on a one-year horizon rather than locking a client into a three-year fixed rate you will regret from the wrong side.

Breaking Filed Aug 28, reported Aug 29–31

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic over training data

The two publishers filed suit on August 28, naming Anthropic along with CEO Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann. The complaint alleges Anthropic obtained copyrighted works through piracy archives including Library Genesis and the Pirate Library Mirror, among them books carrying lyrics and sheet music, and that training on them lets Claude reproduce near-identical compositions. The publishers are seeking a jury trial and statutory damages of up to $150,000 per infringed composition. Anthropic said it disagrees with the claims and intends to defend itself in court.

So what for TSD: Nothing changes in your delivery today, but it is worth knowing that vendor indemnity for AI output is now a live commercial question. When a client asks who is liable if generated copy infringes, the honest answer is that you are responsible for what you ship, so keep AI-generated marketing copy under human review before it goes on a client site.
