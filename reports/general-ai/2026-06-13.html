◆ Open weights keep closing on frontier coding

Model release June 12

Moonshot ships Kimi K2.7 Code — an open 1T-parameter coding model with a 21.8% jump

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2.7 Code, a Mixture-of-Experts model with 1 trillion total parameters (32B active, 384 experts) and a 256K-token context window. The company reports a 21.8% gain on its Kimi Code Bench v2 over the prior K2.6, an 11.0% improvement on Program Bench, and roughly 30% fewer reasoning tokens per task — meaning it gets more done while burning less compute.

The weights are on Hugging Face under a modified-MIT license that permits commercial use with attribution, and it runs on Moonshot's own API. It's the lab's fifth major release in under a year — part of a tight cadence that this week also included Google's open DiffusionGemma 26B (June 10).

So what for TSD: A frontier-grade coding agent you can self-host or rent cheaply is exactly the kind of tool that lowers your per-build cost. Worth benchmarking K2.7 Code against Claude for the grindy, high-volume delivery work (scaffolding, refactors, test generation) where a cheaper model that "gets more done per token" pays off — keep Claude for the judgment-heavy parts.

◆ The capital keeps chasing physical AI

Funding June 10

NEURA Robotics raises up to $1.4B — the largest round ever for a full-stack robotics company

Germany's NEURA Robotics announced a Series C of up to $1.4B at roughly a $7B valuation, backed by NVIDIA, Amazon, Qualcomm, Tether, Bosch, the European Investment Bank and others. NEURA builds "cognitive" robots that keep learning in real-world environments through a shared platform it calls the Neuraverse, and says it wants to ramp to millions of robots by 2030. Its existing orderbook already tops $1B.

So what for TSD: Not a direct play, but a clear read on where the smart money sees AI heading next — out of the browser and into the physical world. For now it mostly tells you that "AI that does real work" is the story enterprises are buying; that framing sells software-only TSD products too.
