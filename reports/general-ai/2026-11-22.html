◆ Models you can build with

Model Nov 17

OpenAI releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, with a 20% price cut on the flagship

Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached and $40 output. Sol stays at

, $0.10 and
0. Both are in the API, ChatGPT Work and Codex.

OpenAI classifies Astra as the first model it has released externally at the High threshold for AI self-improvement, and says it added containment and monitoring for its own research use. The performance claims are OpenAI's and have not been independently confirmed.

So what for TSD: Run your Concierge and Front Desk evals against Sol this week. If it holds quality, you have a second supplier at a known price, which protects retainer margin if one vendor changes terms.

Model Nov 18

Google launches Gemini 4 Neon with a 1-million-token context window

Neon costs $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 cached and

5 output. Google ranks it third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and first on its Coding Agent Index, and says it costs 32% less per task than Gemini 4 Carbon at default settings.

So what for TSD: The long context suits RAG on messy client documents. Test it on your largest Concierge knowledge base before assuming you need retrieval tuning.
Google announcement
Coding Nov 17

Microsoft ships MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash at Ignite

Microsoft says MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5 across four coding benchmarks, at about 40% and 5% of their cost. Those cost figures use a 50% promotional price. Both models are in GitHub Copilot, with Vercel and OpenRouter availability promised and undated.

So what for TSD: Treat the numbers as vendor claims. Worth one real trial on routine refactors once it reaches Vercel's gateway, and no reason to change your main workflow today.

◆ Tools that reach small business

Agents Nov 18 to 20

Claude computer use reaches Team plans while ChatGPT moves into the books

Anthropic opened a computer use beta for Team and Enterprise on Nov 20, rolling out gradually. OpenAI added an Accounting plugin for ChatGPT on Nov 18 that prepares invoice entries, reconciles records and drafts month-end reports.

So what for TSD: Owners will start asking whether AI can run their back office. Have a plain answer ready about what you would automate, what still needs a human check, and where their data goes.
