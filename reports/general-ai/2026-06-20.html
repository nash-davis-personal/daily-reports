◆ Where the AI money is going

Funding June 18, 2026

Baseten closes in on $1.5B as inference becomes the new infrastructure war

AI inference provider Baseten is finalizing a $1.5 billion round at a valuation of up to $13 billion, co-led by Altimeter, Conviction, Spark, Sands, and Wellington, per reports dated June 18. The round is dual-tiered — some investors in at $11B, some at $13B — and lands five months after a $300M Series E at a $5B valuation. The company rents GPU capacity from roughly 20 cloud providers and layers its own inference software on top so customers deploy and fine-tune models on their own data. Annualized revenue reportedly hit ~$600M at the end of Q1, up from ~$200M at the quarter's start.

The signal isn't one startup — it's that "running the model that answers the query" is now a venture-scale category of its own, separate from training and from the model labs.

So what for TSD: Inference is the raw input cost under every Concierge and Front Desk product. A capital flood into inference means cheaper, faster, more reliable serving over the next year — so lock clients into fixed-price Managed retainers now; your token cost falls while the price you charge holds, and the margin is yours.

◆ Coding agents grow up

New capability June 18, 2026

OpenAI gives Codex Record & Replay on macOS

OpenAI's June 18 Codex update added Record & Replay for macOS (excludes the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), plus bulk actions on automation-run history, better SSH connection management, and persistent routing for Browser Use. Record & Replay lets an agent capture a workflow once and re-run it — the same delegation shift the whole coding-agent field is moving toward: you hand off repeatable tasks instead of completions.

So what for TSD: This is the competitor moving the same direction as your Claude Code workflow stack. Worth a 30-minute look — if Record & Replay nails a repetitive piece of your build pipeline (image regen, demo seeding, deploy checks), borrow the pattern even if you stay on Claude Code.

Tooling June 19, 2026 · v2.1.183

Claude Code tightens auto-mode guardrails

The latest Claude Code (v2.1.183, with v2.1.181 alongside) adds auto-mode safety that blocks destructive git and infrastructure commands, deprecation warnings when you request an aging model, a prompt-based /config key=value syntax, and a Bun 1.4 runtime bump. The dedicated changelog briefing covers the details.

So what for TSD: The destructive-command block makes leaving auto mode on for overnight builds materially safer — exactly your scheduled-task pattern. See the Claude Code changelog edition.

◆ Plumbing & access

GA June 17, 2026

Anthropic ships Workload Identity Federation — keyless auth, GA

Anthropic made Workload Identity Federation generally available: keyless authentication across API endpoints, SDKs, and Claude Code, with service accounts so each workload gets its own identity, roles, and audit trail. It removes the need to mint and rotate long-lived API keys for automated jobs.

So what for TSD: Your whole edge is agents running unattended (daily reports, the receptionist, scheduled trades). Keyless service accounts mean fewer raw keys sitting in env files and a real audit trail per job — worth migrating the always-on automations onto when you next touch their auth.
