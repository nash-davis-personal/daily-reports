◆ Compute, models and the China gap

Business Oct 2026

Nvidia says Anthropic's compute contracts now exceed $180 billion

Nvidia disclosed that the contracted value of Anthropic's cloud and neocloud compute deals has passed $180 billion, covering multiple gigawatts of Nvidia capacity deliverable through 2028. Reporting stresses this is a cumulative figure across several providers and equity commitments, not one contract. Gigawatt counts differ between outlets (2.5 to 2.8GW), so treat the exact capacity as unsettled.

So what for TSD: Demand for Claude is being pre-bought years out. That supports building on Claude for client products, and it is a reminder to keep a spend cap and a fallback model in every deployment in case capacity or pricing shifts.

Models Oct 4

The US lead over China's best model is down to about 3%

Bloomberg Intelligence put the US lead over China's top model at roughly 3% on LiveBench after DeepSeek V4.1 Flash scored 81.1, sixth globally. The gap was 9% in May. Separately, Axios reports Nvidia-backed Reflection AI is preparing its first open-weight model aimed at Western enterprises that don't want to run DeepSeek or Qwen.

So what for TSD: Cheap, near-frontier models are becoming a real option for high-volume client work like call transcription and FAQ answering. Price a model-cost review into your Front Desk and Concierge retainers, and benchmark one cheaper model on real client transcripts before quoting.

◆ Agents and the security bill

Security Oct 2026

A Dutch vulnerability-disclosure group was hacked by an autonomous AI agent

The Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure (DIVD) published an account of an intrusion by an autonomous AI agent, which it described as loud and very messy. In the process the group found two zero-days in the Zammad helpdesk software; CVEs are pending. This follows last week's coverage of agents reaching too much on the machines they run on.

So what for TSD: If a client runs Zammad, watch for the CVEs. More generally, add one line to your proposals about rate limiting and alerting on automated traffic, since agent-driven attacks are now documented, not theoretical.
DIVD write-up ↗
Gray market Oct 5

Resellers in China are bulk-opening Claude accounts and reselling access

The Information reports Beijing-based token resellers are collecting identities and USDT-funded cards to open Claude accounts in bulk, routing access through proxies to developers blocked by Anthropic's regional rules. This is secondhand via an aggregator summary; I did not read the original.

So what for TSD: Leaked or shared API keys are the cost risk here. Make sure every client key has a hard monthly cap, is stored server-side only, and is rotated on a schedule.
