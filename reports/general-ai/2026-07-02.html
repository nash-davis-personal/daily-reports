◆ Access & frontier models

New Jul 1, 2026

The US lifted export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 — global access is back

Anthropic restored global access to Claude Fable 5 and its cybersecurity sibling Mythos 5 on July 1, after the Commerce Department lifted an export-control order that had frozen non-US access for nearly three weeks. The block traced to an Amazon research report showing a prompt could bypass some of Fable 5's safeguards; the models returned after Anthropic agreed to proactively detect and report security risks. Fable 5 is live again across the API, Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Cowork, running at 50% of existing usage limits until July 7 before moving to usage-based credits.

So what for TSD: Fable 5 is the strongest coding model on the board (95% SWE-bench Verified) and it's back in Claude Code — worth pointing at your gnarliest build work. But note the July 7 shift to usage-based credits, so meter it before you wire it into anything that runs unattended.

Preview early Jul 2026

OpenAI previews GPT-5.6 as three models — and the cheap one is the story

OpenAI is previewing GPT-5.6 as a family: Sol for frontier reasoning and long-horizon agentic work, Terra as a balanced everyday model pitched at GPT-5.5-level performance for roughly half the cost, and Luna as the fastest, cheapest tier. The split mirrors how Anthropic tiers Opus/Sonnet/Haiku and confirms the pattern that matters to small builders: near-flagship quality keeps getting cheaper on a monthly cadence.

So what for TSD: For the Concierge and Front Desk products, the "Terra"-class tier is the interesting one — same job, roughly half the token cost. When it's GA, re-benchmark your RAG and receptionist prompts against it and Sonnet 5's promo pricing before you lock a model into a client retainer.

◆ Agents, tooling & the money behind them

Market Jul 2026 forecast

Gartner: AI-agent software becomes the fastest-growing slice of enterprise spend

A new Gartner forecast puts AI-agent software spending at about $206.5B in 2026, up 139% from $86.4B in 2025 — the fastest-growing category in enterprise software. The framing is now standard: agents route a user's request to specialized "skills" by matching plain-language descriptions, which is exactly the architecture a scoped SMB agent uses.

So what for TSD: The budget for "an agent that does one job for my business" is real and growing fast. Your edge isn't competing with enterprise platforms — it's shipping a scoped, owned agent (booking, receptionist, lead-router) to a Charlotte SMB in a week. Package one skill, name a price.

Tooling Jul 2026

Coding-agent leaderboard reshuffles again

The current benchmark picture: Codex CLI on GPT-5.5 leads Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 83.4%, with Claude Code on Fable 5 right behind at 83.1% and on Opus 4.8 at 78.9%. On SWE-bench Verified, Fable 5 leads at 95.0% and Opus 4.8 sits at 88.6%. Roughly 65% of working engineers now run two coding agents daily rather than committing to one. Separately, GitHub Copilot's move to usage-based "AI Credits" billing (started June 1) is reopening US individual sign-ups around July 1.

So what for TSD: No single agent wins outright, and the gap between them is small — the leverage is in your harness (skills, loops, verification), not the model badge. Keep Claude Code as the daily driver, but the "run two" pattern is worth a look for hard reviews.
