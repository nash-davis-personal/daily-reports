◆ Builder tools

Launch Nov 23

Anthropic launches Claude Ship, a set of agents that review, fix and approve pull requests

Claude Ship has three parts. Code Review is generally available. PR Steward, which fixes problems on an open pull request, and Claude Approvals, which signs off on routine changes, are in beta. Approvals works only under a policy the team writes and sends riskier changes to a person; Anthropic suggests starting it in observe-only mode. It covers GitHub repositories for Claude Team and Enterprise customers, and running it from the Claude Code CLI bills as normal Claude Code usage.

Anthropic's performance claims are self-reported. TechCrunch and Reuters both carried the launch.

So what for TSD: You are the only reviewer on every client repo, so this is the closest thing to a second engineer. Turn on Code Review for one client repo this week and leave Approvals in observe-only for a month before you let it merge anything. It also gives you a clean line for clients: every change to their site gets a second review before it ships.

Model Nov 23

Z.ai releases GLM-5.4 with a design that reruns 28 middle layers

Two write-ups say Z.ai's new model passes its middle 28 layers through twice, a "Loop Transformer" approach, and that it is available to GLM Coding Plan users, in Z.ai Chat and in the ZCode tool. The general API opens after a security assessment. Performance numbers come from Z.ai and have not been checked independently, and I could not open an official Z.ai post.

So what for TSD: Nothing to switch. Z.ai's coding plans have been the cheap option for running coding agents, so check back once the API opens and an outside benchmark exists.

◆ Platforms and search

Search Nov 23

Google's AI Mode can now draw on Drive, Wallet and the Gemini app

Google extended Personal Intelligence in Search's AI Mode beyond Gmail and Photos to Google Drive, Google Wallet and the Gemini app. Connecting each app is optional and can be switched off at any time. Earlier versions were limited to paying AI Pro and Ultra subscribers on personal accounts.

So what for TSD: Search answers are getting more personal, which means a business shows up when its facts are clear and consistent across its site, its Google profile and its receipts. Keep client sites' hours, services and prices in plain, structured text so AI answers can quote them.

◆ Chips and infrastructure

Leadership Nov 22 to 23

Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, and the stock rises about 31%

Nvidia's 8-K says the board reinstated Huang as president, CEO and director effective Nov 22, eleven days after he resigned. The board said it was satisfied after meetings with him and further discussion of a national security question, which it reviewed with input from senior levels of the U.S. government. The independent investigation continues. The board also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors and is hiring a Chief Compliance Officer. Harvey Jones stays chair and Colette Kress returns to the CFO role.

Nvidia closed Monday up 31.48% and the Nasdaq rose 6.5%.

So what for TSD: The company that supplies most of the compute behind your model vendors has a settled leader again. That reduces one source of supply noise, and it does nothing about the rising hardware prices flagged yesterday.

Earnings Nov 23

HPE posts a record year and raises its fiscal 2027 forecast

HPE's release for the quarter ended Oct 31 reports record operating profit for fiscal 2026 and free cash flow more than three times the prior year's. CEO Antonio Neri credited networking and a bigger role in AI infrastructure; CFO Marie Myers said fiscal 2027 revenue, operating profit and cash flow should be significantly higher. Yesterday's reports that server makers were raising prices on signed orders were not addressed in the release. Dell reports today.

So what for TSD: The hardware sellers are having a great year, which is the other side of rising compute bills. Keep the usage line in Managed AI retainers.
