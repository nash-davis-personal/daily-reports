◆ The enterprise race flipped to Anthropic

Market data Ramp AI Index ◆ April data

Anthropic overtakes OpenAI in US business adoption for the first time

Ramp's AI Index — built from real corporate-card spend across tens of thousands of US businesses — shows Anthropic adoption rising 3.8 points in April to 34.4% of businesses while OpenAI fell 2.9 points to 32.3%. It's the first time Anthropic has led, and it caps a year in which its business adoption roughly quadrupled while OpenAI's grew about 0.3 points. By early 2026 Anthropic was reportedly winning ~70% of head-to-head deals among first-time business buyers.

VentureBeat's read is more cautious: the same data that crowns a leader warns the position may be fragile, threatened by escalating costs, compute constraints, and the token-based pricing that has fueled the revenue growth.

So what for TSD: Your whole stack is built on Claude — and Claude is now the model enterprises are standardizing on. That's a sales asset: "powered by the AI businesses are buying first" belongs on the TSD site and in Grant's leave-behind. But the cost warning is yours too — if Anthropic's pricing climbs, a Managed AI retainer that owns cost control (model routing, caching) protects your margin before clients feel it.

Business Series H ◆ May 28

Anthropic's $965B round and IPO path put it ahead of OpenAI on the public-market clock

Anthropic's $65B Series H closed at a $965B post-money valuation, led by Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia, with run-rate revenue crossing $47B in May. Coverage this week framed a filing path toward an IPO as early as October — which would put the Claude maker on the public market ahead of OpenAI.

So what for TSD: The vendor you depend on is now the most valuable private AI company and is capitalized for years. That's a durable bet to build a business on. The flip side is pricing power — a company funding a multi-gigawatt compute buildout will keep token prices where its economics need them, so design client pricing with headroom, not on today's rate card.

◆ The model wave that hasn't broken yet

Pending June 2026

Gemini 3.5 Pro GA still pending; Sonnet 4.8 and Grok 5 rumored behind it

Google announced Gemini 3.5 Pro at I/O on May 19 with a June GA target — a 2-million-token context window and a "Deep Think" reasoning mode — but as of this weekend it's still in limited Vertex preview, with Pichai telling developers to wait. A rumored Claude Sonnet 4.8 and xAI's long-delayed Grok 5 are also expected in the same window. For now, Claude Opus 4.8 still leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61.4, ahead of GPT-5.5 (60.2) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (57).

So what for TSD: Don't re-benchmark or re-platform client products mid-wave. Opus 4.8 is still the quality leader; wait for Gemini 3.5 Pro and Sonnet 4.8 to actually ship, then run one honest eval against your real workloads. A 2M-token context, if it lands cheap, is a genuine unlock for the Concierge RAG product — worth a test when GA is real.
