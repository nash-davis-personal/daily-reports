◆ Models and pricing

Model Oct 13

Gemini 4 Argon reaches general availability with a discount that expires Dec 1

Google made gemini-4-argon generally available on the paid Gemini API tier and in AI Studio. Secondary coverage reports introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through Nov 30, rising to $4 and $20 on Dec 1. It is not on the free tier. I could not reach Google's own pricing page, so confirm the numbers there before quoting a client.

So what for TSD: If a client feature is cost-sensitive, run your eval set against Argon before Dec 1 and price the project on the post-discount rate, not the promo one.

Open weights Oct 15

Meta releases Muse Spark Open, its strongest open-weight model

The model has roughly 816B total parameters with about 42B active per token and a context window up to 1M tokens. Weights are on Hugging Face, Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden and Microsoft Foundry. Meta's model-card numbers show it leading on MCP-Atlas (89.2) and trailing Kimi K3 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (76.4 vs 85.0). The license is the Muse Model License, which has limited commercial terms, and Meta says a full evaluation report is still coming.

So what for TSD: Read the license before you pitch "self-hosted, you own it" to a client. It is useful as a price check against hosted models, not yet as a default.

Pricing Oct 17

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash-1017 keeps its price; Search and Fetch APIs go GA

The snapshot update keeps the deepseek-flash name and token prices ($0.15 in / $0.60 out off-peak, $0.30 / $1.20 at peak) and ships MIT-licensed weights. DeepSeek says outputs are shorter and tasks finish faster. Its Search and Fetch APIs cost $0.75 per 1,000 calls per endpoint, and calls made through server-side tools in DeepSeek Harness, Claude Code and Codex are free until Oct 31, 16:00 UTC.

So what for TSD: Cheap model plus free search through Oct 31 makes a good test bed for the research-style features in TSD Concierge. Vercel AI Gateway already lists the model (see Technology).

◆ Money and markets

Funding Oct 19–21

OpenAI is reported to be chasing $30B at above $1.4T before Anthropic's IPO

Reuters-sourced reports say SoftBank and MGX are in talks, and that OpenAI told investors annualized revenue is about $50 billion. The same coverage puts Anthropic's Q3 revenue at $16.8 billion versus $11.6 billion in Q2 and says some investors want a listing valuation near $1.5T. All of it is unnamed-source reporting; neither company has confirmed terms.

So what for TSD: Capital is flowing into frontier labs while AI stocks wobble. Client AI budgets are not affected today, but vendor pricing power is worth watching into November.
