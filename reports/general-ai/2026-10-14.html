◆ Frontier and small models

NewOct 7

Claude Haiku 5.5: the price floor moves again

Anthropic released Haiku 5.5 on Oct 7 at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100K tokens ($0.50 / $2.50 above that). That is roughly 75% below Haiku 4.5. It is the first Haiku with adjustable effort settings (Low through Max) and targets classification, summarization, extraction, live support and voice agents.

So what for TSD: Re-price the unit economics on TSD Front Desk and Concierge. A receptionist call or a RAG answer on Haiku 5.5 costs a rounding error, so margin on a flat monthly retainer widens. Test it as the default model for intent routing and FAQ answers, and keep a bigger model only for escalations.

LaunchOct 12

Meta Muse Wonder: Meta enters the frontier tier

Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, aimed at long-running agent work. Active Memory carries information across conversations and Active Focus keeps the model working through hard tasks and checking in before important actions. Both take a 1M-token context with text, image, video and audio input. Muse Wonder is on the Meta Model API and Muse Code at $4 / $20 per million tokens; Full is for Muse Code subscribers.

So what for TSD: It is priced like Opus-class models, so it competes on quality rather than cost. Nothing to adopt today. Worth one eval run against your Concierge test set if the API access is open to you.

PreviewOct 6

Mistral Large 4: 1.05T-parameter open-weight model, weights coming

Mistral opened a public preview of Large 4 on Oct 6 through its API. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 1.05 trillion total parameters and roughly 50 billion active. Downloadable weights are promised by the end of October. No license has been published, so "open-weight" has no terms behind it yet.

So what for TSD: Watch the license before planning anything. If it permits commercial use, it is a candidate for a client who needs data to stay on their own infrastructure.

◆ Business and platforms

BusinessOct 14

Anthropic investor day ahead of a possible November IPO

Anthropic invited institutional investors to its San Francisco headquarters today and is expected to share third-quarter figures for the first time. Reporting puts investor expectations at $1.8T to $2T, about twice the $965B Series H mark from May, with a marketing launch as early as the week of Nov 9. No senior Anthropic executive has publicly set a target, and no prospectus is public yet.

So what for TSD: You build on Claude, so company stability and pricing power matter. A public Anthropic means quarterly pressure on margins and pricing, so keep your prompts portable and your cost tracking per client.

PricingSep 29

OpenAI DevDay: halved GPT-6 prices, always-on "dots", $500 Pro tier

At DevDay on Sep 29 OpenAI cut GPT-6 Sol to $2 / $10 and Luna to $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens, announced GPT-6.1 Sol with stronger agentic coding, and launched always-on personal agents called dots that run on their own cloud computers and connect to 4,000+ apps. A $500-per-month Pro 500 plan adds a faster tier, and the existing $200 Pro plan allowance is halved from Oct 30. This is outside the Oct 8 window but was not covered in an earlier edition.

So what for TSD: Dots show where client expectations are heading: an assistant that acts across Slack, calendars and email. That is the pitch for a Managed AI retainer. If you run Codex on the $200 plan, expect less headroom after Oct 30.
