◆ Money and compute

FundingOct 19 (Reuters)

OpenAI is reportedly racing to close a $30B round at a valuation above $1.4T

Reuters reports OpenAI wants the round done before Anthropic's IPO, which the same coverage dates to mid-November. A separate Reuters item from Oct 15 says investors pushed Anthropic's IPO valuation down to about $1.5T from $2T over Q3 growth concerns. Both are single-source reports relayed through a news digest; neither company has confirmed the figures in what I could read.

So what for TSD: Capital at this scale keeps model prices subsidized for now. Do not build client pricing on today's token rates staying flat; keep each AI feature behind a swappable model setting.

ComputeOct 16–19 (Bloomberg, FT)

Anthropic is financing a lot of chips: a second Nscale deal and a 10% yield debt tranche

Bloomberg reports Nscale and Anthropic are in talks on a second deal worth about $45B, 460 MW of Vera Rubin capacity on a West Virginia campus. The FT reported Oct 16 that Anthropic is offering Blackstone a 10%+ yield on a $9B tranche of $60B in Broadcom-arranged TPU debt. Anthropic also bought Opaque Systems (confidential computing) and hired its CEO and CTO, per SecurityWeek.

So what for TSD: Debt at 10% yields is a reminder that AI capacity is expensive money. Expect rate limits and price moves to follow the financing, which is one more reason to quote clients with headroom.

◆ Agents in the wild

ToolingOct 17

Claude Code v2.1.302 adds an official plugin system

The release also turns fork subagents on by default in the SDK and apps (GitHub release, relayed by aiweekly). Plugins give a supported way to package your own skills, commands and hooks and hand them to other people.

So what for TSD: Package your report/podcast workflow, your Next.js deploy checklist and your client-onboarding prompts as a plugin. A plugin you can install on a client's machine is also a deliverable you can charge for.
v2.1.302 summary
AccessOct 17 (The Verge)

Amazon, Yelp and Delta join banks in blocking third-party AI agents

The Verge reports the three companies now block outside AI agents from everyday tasks such as shopping, reviews and travel changes. Separately, Meta said last week's Claude-linked scraper triggered an Instagram suspension (covered Oct 19).

So what for TSD: If TSD Front Desk or Booking Bridge acts on a customer's behalf on someone else's site, expect failures. Use official APIs and partner integrations, and tell clients up front which flows can break.
The Verge via aiweekly
OversightOct 15–19

The FTC sends 50-page demands to OpenAI and Anthropic, and a hacking campaign is tied to coding agents

Axios reports the FTC issued civil investigative demands over AI safety incidents on Oct 15. Reuters, as relayed by aiweekly, reports a China-linked campaign breached 100+ firms for about $5K using Claude Code and DeepSeek agents; the digest's link slug attributes the figure to a firm called Norforge, so treat the attribution as unclear.

So what for TSD: Clients will start asking what your AI features log and who can call them. Have a one-page answer on key storage, spend caps and audit logs ready before they ask.
