◆ The models came back — with a government gate

Live again July 1, 2026

Claude Fable 5 is available globally again

After roughly 18 days offline under U.S. export controls, Anthropic restored Fable 5 worldwide on July 1 — Claude.ai, the Claude Platform API, Claude Code, and Cowork. Axios' July 3 reconstruction lays out how the models were revived behind the scenes, and the Commerce Department's June 30 move to lift the restriction that had blocked foreign-national access.

So what for TSD: Every TSD product that rides Claude — the receptionist, the RAG concierge, this briefing engine — is back on full footing. But the blackout is the lesson: build a documented fallback model path (Sonnet 5 stayed up; keep an OpenAI/Gemini adapter warm) so a client demo never dies because one lab goes dark for a policy reason.

Axios — How the world's top AI models were revived ↗
Policy early July 2026

The White House is slow-rolling GPT-5.6, one customer at a time

Following the same federal pressure that pulled Anthropic's top models, Washington asked OpenAI to hold broad release of GPT-5.6 and limit early access to a small set of approved partners. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick reportedly warned Altman against a public release without agency sign-off; Altman told staff the government is "approving access customer by customer," with broad access hoped for mid-to-late July. The stated concern is cybersecurity — the same coding and bug-finding power that helps builders helps attackers.

So what for TSD: Frontier capability is now rate-limited by policy, not just by the labs. That's a moat for implementers: the newest raw model matters less than who can reliably ship a working product on today's approved models. Sell delivery and reliability, and stop pinning proposals to a specific unreleased model.

◆ Follow the money

Business July 2, 2026

Anthropic's revenue is compounding faster than the headlines

Fortune reports Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI on self-reported revenue and is tracking toward roughly $47B in annualized run-rate — up from about $9B at the end of 2025 — with a path to profitability in 2029, a year ahead of OpenAI's projected timeline. The growth is being driven heavily by API and coding usage.

So what for TSD: The platform TSD is built on is winning on exactly the workload TSD sells (code + API automation). That's a story to put in front of prospects — "we build on the fastest-growing enterprise AI platform" — and a reason to keep going deeper on Claude Code and skills as the core delivery edge.
Fortune — Altman seeks a new world order as OpenAI loses ground ↗
Funding July 1, 2026

Together AI banks $800M to make open models cheap to run

Together AI raised an $800M Series C at an $8.3B valuation, led by Aramco Ventures with Nvidia, General Catalyst, Vista and others participating. The pitch is an open-model cloud — cheaper inference for developers and enterprises who don't want to pay frontier-lab prices for every call.

So what for TSD: Cheaper open-model inference is a margin lever. For high-volume, low-stakes tasks (lead classification, first-draft copy, tagging) an open model on a cloud like this can undercut per-token cost while Claude handles the client-facing reasoning. Worth a pricing experiment on the sales-loop and RAG workloads.
