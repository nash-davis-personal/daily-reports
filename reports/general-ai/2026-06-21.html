◆ The builder layer Nash ships on

Tooling June 2026 release notes

Anthropic puts a control plane over MCP connectors, starting with Okta

Per Anthropic's June release notes, Claude now supports enterprise-managed MCP connector access — an admin can centrally authorize which connectors are available across Claude chat, Claude Code, and Cowork, beginning with Okta. It's a governance layer on top of the same Model Context Protocol that lets Claude reach external tools and data.

MCP crossing into managed, audited territory is the quiet tell that the connector layer is maturing from hobby plumbing into something an IT department will sign off on.

So what for TSD: Every Concierge and Front Desk build sits on this connector layer. When a bigger SMB or a regulated client asks "who controls what the AI can touch," the answer is now a real feature, not a hand-wave — that removes a real objection from the sales conversation.
Anthropic / Claude release notes, June 2026
Tooling June 2026

Claude Design crosses 1M users in week one, tightens its Claude Code sync

Anthropic says over a million people used Claude Design in its first week. The June update keeps generated work aligned with a project's design system across files, adds direct canvas editing, stronger layout controls, and a tighter handoff into Claude Code.

So what for TSD: Design-system-aware generation that round-trips into Claude Code is exactly the loop Nash runs solo — mock a client site, keep it on-brand, ship it. Faster front-end iteration is more demo sites built per night without hiring anyone.

◆ Models & the buildout

Model June 2026

Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash aims a cheap model at agents and coding

Google is pitching Gemini 3.5 Flash as "frontier performance for agents and coding" at Flash-tier pricing — part of the same agentic push that included its Antigravity tooling. It's a fast, low-cost option for high-volume agent calls rather than a reasoning flagship.

So what for TSD: A genuinely cheap, capable model is the right tool for the chatty, high-call-count paths in a RAG Concierge where Claude's depth is overkill. Worth a head-to-head on cost-per-conversation for the demo concierges — multi-model routing protects margin on fixed-price retainers.

Infrastructure June 18, 2026

FERC moves to fast-track AI data centers — power, not chips, is the ceiling

In a unanimous 5-0 vote on June 18, FERC issued "show-cause" orders to the six largest U.S. grid operators — PJM, MISO, SPP, CAISO, ISO New England, and NYISO — giving them 60 days to justify or rewrite how large loads like AI data centers connect to the grid. The orders touch regions serving about 200 million Americans across 30+ states. Texas, on its own grid, is exempt.

The framing matters: the constraint on AI capacity has shifted from GPU supply to whether the grid can deliver the megawatts. Last week's briefings flagged water as a data-center input nobody priced; this is the same story for electricity.

So what for TSD: Not directly actionable, but it's the macro reason inference prices won't fall forever — power is the floor under token costs. Lock clients into fixed-price Managed retainers now while serving costs are still drifting down; you keep the margin if they later tick back up.
