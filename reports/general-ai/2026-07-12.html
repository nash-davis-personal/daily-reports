◆ The money is rotating into inference

New Jul 8, 2026

SambaNova raises $1B at an $11B valuation as an Nvidia-inference challenger

SambaNova completed the first close of a $1 billion Series F on July 8, valuing the company at $11 billion post-money, led by General Atlantic with Seligman Ventures, T. Rowe Price and Capital Group participating. It comes only about five months after its last large round. The company builds custom AI chips, systems and cloud aimed squarely at inference — running models cheaply at scale, rather than training them.

It fits the week's broader tape: SK Hynix's record US listing, Together AI's $800M raise on July 1, and a semiconductor industry on track for roughly $975B in sales this year. Capital is chasing the layer underneath the models — the compute and memory that make cheap tokens possible.

So what for TSD: More inference competition means the per-token cost of the AI you resell keeps falling. That's a margin tailwind, not a threat: price your AI offers (Front Desk, Concierge) on the value delivered, and let each new cheap-inference entrant widen the gap between what you charge and what you pay. Keep model calls behind an adapter so you can chase the cheapest capable provider without a rewrite.

◆ Agents get cheaper to reach

Access Jul 9–12, 2026

OpenAI's ChatGPT Work agent expands from Pro toward Plus and Business

ChatGPT Work — the agent OpenAI shipped July 9 that gathers context across a user's apps, breaks a goal into steps, and returns finished sheets, slides, docs and small web apps with Codex-powered editing — launched on Pro, Enterprise and Edu and is rolling out to Plus and Business "in the next few days." It runs on the GPT-5.6 family, whose cheapest tier (Luna) is $1 input / $6 output per 1M tokens.

The direction matters more than the feature. The "does the whole task, not just answers" tier is moving from a $200/month audience to a $20/month one. Your SMB clients' employees will start doing this themselves inside a chat window.

So what for TSD: The moat is not "can build a doc/site" — that's commoditizing fast. It's integration and accountability: an AI wired into a specific business's phone line, calendar, CRM and site, that someone (you) keeps working. Position TSD offers as "done and maintained for your business," not "AI can make things," because the generic version of the latter just got cheaper for everyone.
