◆ The business of the labs

Legal Filed Nov 27

OpenAI countersues Apple, alleging stolen trade secrets and a supplier blockade

OpenAI filed a countersuit against Apple in a California court on Friday. It accuses Apple of taking its trade secrets and of blocking its access to hardware suppliers. This answers Apple's July suit, which accused OpenAI and two former Apple employees of misappropriating Apple trade secrets for OpenAI's consumer-hardware push. Apple called the counterclaims an attempt to distract from its own case.

So what for TSD: No change to anything you ship. The fight is over OpenAI's device plans, so the APIs you call are untouched. Keep Claude as the default and OpenAI as a fallback, which is already how Front Desk is built.
CNN Al Jazeera Reuters via Investing.com
Markets Nov 27

Anthropic shares recover above the $83 IPO price, while bond buyers expect a junk rating

Anthropic stock rose on Friday after trading below its $83 offer price earlier in the week. Seeking Alpha put the move at about 9%, to $90.06. Investing.com reported a smaller morning gain to $85.80 and tied it to dip-buying in AI stocks after Thanksgiving. The two figures differ, so treat the exact price as unsettled. Wedbush and Canaccord Genuity started coverage with targets of $120 and $135.

Seeking Alpha also reports that bond investors expect the company's debt to be rated junk because of its spending plans. Anthropic's own release says the IPO closed at about 1.13 billion Class A shares with the overallotment, for roughly $93.5 billion in gross proceeds.

So what for TSD: Your main model vendor now reports quarterly and carries a lot of debt. Price changes and rate-limit policy will now show up in earnings calls. Keep model choice behind one config value in every client build so a price move costs you a deploy and nothing more.

◆ Builder tooling

Tool Nov 27

Hub launches as an open-source dispatcher for Claude Code and Codex sessions

Hub, listed on Product Hunt and picked up by DevHunt's Nov 27 digest, is an open-source tool for starting and tracking several Claude Code and Codex sessions from one place. It joins a crowded field of agent session managers. The listing does not give usage numbers.

So what for TSD: You already run several sessions at once for client work. Spend 20 minutes on it this weekend. If it beats your current setup, keep it. If it doesn't, the thing to copy for your own tooling is the single view of every agent's status.
