◆ Agents are now a line item

Revenue September 25

Cognition says Devin passed $1B in annualized revenue

Bloomberg reports Cognition's run rate crossed $1B this month, up from $492M in May and just over $900M earlier in September. Devin is used at GE Aerospace, Rivian, Rohlik and Exa, and the company raised more than $2B at a $48B valuation this month.

Run-rate numbers flatter fast-growing companies, but the direction is clear: businesses are paying real money for agents that write code without a person typing it.

So what for TSD: Buyers now accept "an agent does the work" as a product. Pitch Front Desk and Concierge as staff capacity with a monthly price, and stop explaining the technology first.
Bloomberg ↗
Launch September 25

Microsoft rebuilt Copilot around Home, Code and Autopilot

Home merges chat and Cowork and edits Word, Excel and PowerPoint in place. Code lets non-developers build apps and automations in plain language on GitHub Copilot tech, and VentureBeat reports Microsoft will host those generated apps. Autopilot is a persistent agent you give a name, role and goal; it watches channels, follows up and runs recurring work in Microsoft 365 with nobody prompting it.

Home and Code roll out to Frontier-program companies in the coming weeks. Autopilot enters private preview by month's end. Satya Nadella said enterprise agents could become a market far larger than cloud.

So what for TSD: Your SMB clients on Microsoft 365 will hear "just have Copilot build it." Position TSD as the person who finishes and hosts what they start, and keep customer-facing work (sites, phones, booking) as your lane.

◆ Anthropic's Friday

Court September 25

An appeals court kept the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk label on Anthropic

A 2–1 D.C. Circuit panel (Judges Katsas and Rao, with Henderson dissenting) upheld one of two Pentagon designations that bar the military and its contractors from using Claude. A San Francisco federal judge struck down the parallel designation in August, so this ruling leaves one in force.

Anthropic said it disagrees and is "considering all options, including further review." The dispute began when talks on a $200M contract broke down over Anthropic's limits on autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance.

So what for TSD: No effect on commercial Claude use. If a prospect does defense contracting, ask early whether they can use Claude-backed tools, and keep your stack able to swap models.
CNBC ↗
Business September 25

$11.6B to Akamai for CPUs, and a request for founder voting control

Anthropic committed $11.6B over seven years to Akamai's cloud, potentially growing to about $20B, and got a warrant for up to about 5% of Akamai stock that vests as it spends. The capacity is mostly CPUs, the chips that run the code and browsing agents do; first services are planned for late Q2 2027.

Separately, Anthropic asked shareholders to give its seven co-founders 50.1% of voting power through a special share class ahead of an IPO, modeled on Palantir's structure. The shares carry no extra economic rights.

So what for TSD: Agents are pushing demand toward ordinary compute, which is good news for the cost of running tool-heavy agents like Concierge over time. The IPO governance piece changes nothing for you today.

◆ Voice and next week

Pricing September 25

Alibaba shipped Qwen-Audio 3.1 and cut voice API prices up to 95%

Five models: ASR, TTS and Realtime upgrades plus ASR-Next (speakers, timestamps, emotion, background sound) and TTS-Next (voices, effects and ambience together). ASR prices fall up to 95%, TTS about 70% and Realtime about 85%. ASR covers 30 languages with about 160ms to first character.

So what for TSD: Price pressure on voice is real. Benchmark it against your current Front Desk stack for cost per minute, but check where audio is processed before routing any US client's calls through it.
The Decoder ↗
Upcoming September 29

OpenAI DevDay is Monday, with a GPT-6 Cyber preview and a dozen-plus launches

Fortune reports OpenAI will preview GPT-6 Cyber with a companion deployment product and ship a dozen or more other products around the event, most unrelated to security. Forbes has reported managed agents for office work are on the list. The keynote streams free.

So what for TSD: Block 30 minutes Monday. Anything that makes hosted agents cheaper or easier changes what you can quote for Front Desk and Concierge.
