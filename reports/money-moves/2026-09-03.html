2. Charge for enforced agent permissions instead of giving them away

The shift: Per-action agent authorization became a product category this week. JetStream shipped Clearance, which evaluates and approves every agent tool call before it executes and blocks exfiltration-shaped sequences; AccuKnox shipped AgentZ with sandboxes, role-based access, runtime credential injection and audit traces; Genesys added a policy-aware AI Control Plane for contact-center agents. source ↗

Why you: The single hardest objection in an AI receptionist sale is "what stops it from doing something expensive." Right now your answer is a promise. Enterprises are buying a mechanism, which means a mechanism is now a sellable line item at SMB scale too, and you can build it in one evening because Front Desk and Booking Bridge are yours end to end.
First move: Write down the explicit allow-list of actions Front Desk and Booking Bridge may take, enforce it in code before the tool call rather than in the prompt, and log every attempt including denials. Screenshot the audit log and add a "Guarded Actions" line to the Managed AI retainer with its own price.
