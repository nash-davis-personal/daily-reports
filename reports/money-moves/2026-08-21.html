The shift: OpenAI shipped an Apple Messages plug-in on August 20 that lets ChatGPT draft, send, delete and search texts — and warned users in the same announcement not to enable persistent approval because it "removes your final chance to review a message before ChatGPT sends it." Binance shipped an agent trading product with a similar shape a day earlier. source ↗

Why you: Big vendors are normalizing agents that text on your behalf while publicly flagging the risk. That creates demand for the supervised version, and supervision is a product feature you can build in a weekend: drafted follow-ups queued for one-tap owner approval, with an audit log.
