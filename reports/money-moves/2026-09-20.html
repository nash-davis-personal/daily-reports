Voice agents got cheap enough to be a retainer, and the Fed just made projects harder to sell

Re-cost TSD Front Desk on Gemini 3.8 Live and launch a cheap entry tier this week

Google shipped Gemini 3.8 Live on September 15: native speech-to-speech, tool calls running in the background while the model keeps talking, automatic switching across 97 languages, and the top spot on the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech index at 82.6.
