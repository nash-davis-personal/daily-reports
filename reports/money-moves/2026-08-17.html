The shift: Stripe agreed to buy OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, five times its May valuation, to own the routing and metering layer between eight million developers and 400-plus models. The payments company that already meters the internet just bought the meter for AI. source ↗

Why you: This is the cheapest move on the list because it creates revenue without creating work. Flat project pricing on an AI product means you eat every extra conversation, and your margin quietly erodes as the client succeeds. First move: Pull the last 60 days of conversation counts for every client on Front Desk or Concierge. Find the median, set the floor just above it, and set a per-conversation rate past that. Apply it to the next new quote this week rather than renegotiating existing clients mid-term, then move each existing client at renewal. One quote, one paragraph, zero build.
