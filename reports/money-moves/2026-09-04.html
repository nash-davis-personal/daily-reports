In the same 48 hours, JetStream, Tenable and Proofpoint all shipped products whose entire premise is gating what an agent is allowed to do. source ↗

Why you: Two of your three products talk directly to end customers, so you have to do this work anyway. Deliver it free to your two best existing clients as a goodwill pass, then price it at a flat fee for everyone else and fold ongoing review into the retainer.

6. Prototype a 60-second quote generator against the computer-use jump

The shift: GPT-6 Astra launched September 3 and its clearest separation is not coding but automation and computer use — 41.4% on AutomationBench against 26.9% for Opus 5 and 31.4% for Fable 5.1 — with an explicit pitch about creating documents, spreadsheets and presentations from templates. API access is promised "in the coming days" at $10 input and $50 output per million. source ↗

Why you: Quoting is the unglamorous bottleneck in every trades business you talk to — a job walk turns into an evening of typing, and slow quotes lose work. You already have the intake surface (Front Desk transcripts, Booking Bridge) and the template skills. This is a genuinely new capability rather than a repackage, which is the category the profile says to chase. Be honest that the benchmark was run at maximum effort and the API is not open yet, so this is a prototype bet rather than a September revenue line.
First move: Do the paper version first, this week, with the model you already have. Take one real client's last ten quotes, build a template, and generate quote number eleven from a recorded intake call. If the output is close enough that the owner would send it with light edits, you have a product; then swap in Astra when the API opens and see if the automation gap is real. If the paper version fails, drop it and lose nothing.
