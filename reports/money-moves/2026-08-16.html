Turn dependency patching into a paid monthly line on every client you already have

A CVSS 10.0 remote code execution flaw in SAP Commerce Cloud was patched August 12 and was being exploited against honeypots by August 14 — with no public proof-of-concept, meaning attackers built the exploit from the patch itself in under 72 hours. In the same window, Z.ai's open-weights GLM-5.3 was handed a reverse-engineering task and found a real vulnerability in Cursor. You already patch client sites informally and eat the hours. Package it: a fixed monthly for dependency monitoring, patch application, runtime upgrades and an uptime check, sold to the clients whose sites you built. Nothing to build, no new prospect to find, and it converts unpaid maintenance guilt into recurring revenue.
