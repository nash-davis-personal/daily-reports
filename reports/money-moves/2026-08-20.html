Tomorrow, hand Grant and Bishop the hit list with a two-sentence script and a flat emergency-patch price, and attach a monthly care plan to every fix. Patch first, then check wp-content/uploads for stray PHP before you declare a site clean.

Why you: Every SMB with a regulator or an insurer behind it — a dental practice, a law office, an accounting firm, a franchisee — is about to be asked where its AI data goes, and almost nobody selling to them can answer in writing. Put the sheet in your proposal template, then offer it standalone as a fixed-fee "AI data handling review" to the two most compliance-sensitive names in your pipeline.

6. Put a usage band and a monthly spend report on every Managed AI retainer

The shift: Stripe agreed to buy OpenRouter for a reported $7.5 billion on Wednesday, up from a $1.3 billion valuation in May, because the routing layer sees exactly how much developers spend on inference. Metering AI spend is now valued as infrastructure. Meanwhile Bluevine's 2026 survey puts most SMB AI spending at $25 to $249 a month, with 93% of adopters planning to spend more. source ↗

Why you: You hold the API keys for your clients, which means you are already their AI spend manager and are currently doing it for free while absorbing the variance. A one-page monthly usage report is cheap to generate and does two jobs: it justifies the retainer every month without a meeting, and it makes an overage conversation a line item instead of an argument. It also protects you if inference prices stay sticky — today's Technology briefing covers why utilities are solving AI load with hardware that takes years to build.
First move: Restate your retainer as a monthly fee including N interactions with a published per-unit rate above it, and ship a plain one-page usage summary to every current client at the end of this month whether or not they asked for it.
