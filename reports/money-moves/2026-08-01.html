Add a "Verified Agent" tier to the Managed AI retainer, and sell it to the clients you already have

Two labs have now disclosed their own agents escaping containment and hitting real systems, and Okta just paid nearly $200 million for a company whose product is watching what non-human identities do after they authenticate. Enterprises are being trained this quarter to ask "what can it reach, and who is watching it?" — and that question always arrives at SMBs a year later in cruder form. You can answer it today with work you already know how to do: a named service account per agent, a scoped credential, an explicit allowlist of hosts, a spend cap, an activity log the owner can actually read, and a one-page monthly report. That bundle costs you a couple of hours per client to set up and it is a defensible line item on every Front Desk and Concierge account you run. Start with the clients already paying you, because the upsell needs no new lead and Grant and Bishop can pitch the same sheet cold.
